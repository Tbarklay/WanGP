# BINGO, Follow Along (about 2:45)

The classic BINGO song, in the same 3D farm style as our other videos. Obi plays Bingo, the farmer's dog. Each verse
drops one more letter, and before each new verse the farmer TELLS the kids what to do instead of the missing letters
(round 2 clap, round 3 stomp, round 4 pat your head,
round 5 jump, and last of all bark like Bingo). The very next shot shows the farmer doing it, facing the
camera, so kids can copy him, and then the verse is sung with that action in place of the letters.

The song is sung by a male voice. The farmer's spoken calls are a separate spoken male voice, placed in a short
instrumental break before each verse, with his mouth lip-synced to them with InfiniteTalk.
Lyrics are the traditional public-domain song with action words in place of the dropped letters.
Version 2: no sheep (its look kept changing); Obi's ears must stay floppy in every shot.
Title idea: BINGO! 🐶👏 Clap, Stomp & Jump Along | Bingo Dog Song for Kids | Sing with Obi

## Music
Cheerful children's sing-along, bright and bouncy country folk, warm friendly male vocal with a children's choir
spelling the letters, the action words sung loud and punchy on the beat, acoustic guitar, banjo, fiddle, hand claps,
120 BPM, major key, a short 4-second instrumental break before every verse, kids music

## Sound effects
clap: three crisp hand claps in a row, clean, no music
stomp: three heavy cartoon boot stomps on a wooden floor, clean, no music
jump: a cartoon spring boing, playful, clean, no music

## Voices

call_pat: ryan | cheerful friendly country farmer, playful and silly | Now let's pat our heads! Pat, pat, pat!
call_jump: ryan | cheerful friendly country farmer, very excited | Ooh, this time, let's jump! Jump up high!
call_woof: ryan | cheerful friendly country farmer, laughing | Last time! Let's all bark like Bingo! Woof woof!
