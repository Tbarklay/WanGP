# BINGO, Follow Along (version 3, about 2:00 plus the intro and goodbye cards)

The classic BINGO song in our 3D farm style. Obi plays Bingo, the farmer's dog. Before each new round the farmer
calls out what to do instead of the missing letters (round 2 clap, round 3 stomp, round 4 pat your head, round 5
jump, round 6 bark like Bingo), then shows it.

Version 3 is a real follow-along:
- While the letters are sung, the screen stays on ONE steady shot: the farmer and Obi side by side, full body,
  facing the camera like a teacher, doing the action. No cutaways during the spelling, so kids always have someone
  to copy.
- Giant colored letters B-I-N-G-O run across the top during the spelling (the [spell:] lines), the sung letter
  drawn bigger. Each round one more letter turns into the action's picture (clap hands, boot, hand, frog, puppy),
  so kids SEE which letters are gone. The top third of every spelling shot is plain sky to make room for them.
- No frozen frames: each lip-synced call is followed straight away by the demo shot, which fills the rest of the
  instrumental break.
- The song starts right away: no slow scenery shot at the start (the intro card opens the video).
The song, the farmer's calls and the lip-synced call clips from version 2 are kept; only the pictures change.
Title idea: BINGO! 🐶👏 Clap, Stomp & Jump Along | Bingo Dog Song for Kids | Sing with Obi

## Music
Cheerful children's sing-along, bright and bouncy country folk, warm friendly male vocal with a children's choir
spelling the letters, the action words sung loud and punchy on the beat, acoustic guitar, banjo, fiddle, hand claps,
120 BPM, major key, a short 4-second instrumental break before every verse, kids music

## Sound effects
clap: three crisp hand claps in a row, clean, no music
stomp: three heavy cartoon boot stomps on a wooden floor, clean, no music
jump: a cartoon spring boing, playful, clean, no music
