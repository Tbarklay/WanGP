# BINGO (about 2:15)

The classic BINGO song. Obi plays Bingo, the farmer's dog. Each verse swaps one more letter for a clap, with the
farmer and the farm animals clapping along.

Lyrics are the traditional public-domain song.
Title idea: B-I-N-G-O! 🐶👏 Bingo Dog Song for Kids | Sing with Obi

## Music
Cheerful children's sing-along, bright and bouncy country folk, warm friendly male vocal with a children's choir
spelling the letters, acoustic guitar, banjo, fiddle, big hand claps replacing the missing letters, 120 BPM,
major key, kids music
