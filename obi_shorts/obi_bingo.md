# Obi's Spelling Song: B-I-N-G-O, then O-B-I! (about 2:45)

Sunny version. Bingo is the farmer's big old sheepdog, and puppy Obi wants a name song of his own. Each verse spells
B-I-N-G-O and swaps one more letter for a clap, with the farm animals clapping along. In the finale the farmer
teaches Obi to spell his own name, O-B-I.

Original lyrics written for this channel (the letter-clapping game is a classic, but the words here are our own).
Title idea: B-I-N-G-O! 🐶👏 Spell and Clap with Obi | Bingo Song for Kids

## Music
Cheerful children's sing-along, bright and bouncy country pop, warm friendly male vocal with a children's choir
spelling the letters, acoustic guitar, banjo, fiddle, big hand claps on every clapped letter, 120 BPM, major key,
kids music

## Lyrics
[Intro]
Out on the farm there lives a dog, the farmer's oldest friend
His name is Bingo, big and kind, let's spell it end to end

[Verse 1]
B-I-N-G-O, B-I-N-G-O, B-I-N-G-O
That's how we spell his name, oh!

[Verse 2]
(clap)-I-N-G-O, (clap)-I-N-G-O, (clap)-I-N-G-O
Clap the first one, here we go!

[Verse 3]
(clap)-(clap)-N-G-O, (clap)-(clap)-N-G-O, (clap)-(clap)-N-G-O
Clap two letters, nice and slow!

[Verse 4]
(clap)-(clap)-(clap)-G-O, (clap)-(clap)-(clap)-G-O, (clap)-(clap)-(clap)-G-O
Three big claps, now steal the show!

[Verse 5]
(clap)-(clap)-(clap)-(clap)-O, (clap)-(clap)-(clap)-(clap)-O, (clap)-(clap)-(clap)-(clap)-O
Four claps and a single O!

[Verse 6]
(clap)-(clap)-(clap)-(clap)-(clap), (clap)-(clap)-(clap)-(clap)-(clap), (clap)-(clap)-(clap)-(clap)-(clap)
Bingo, Bingo, way to go!

[Bridge]
Now little Obi wags his tail, he wants a name song too
Three small letters, short and sweet, let's spell it just for you

[Outro]
O-B-I, O-B-I, O-B-I
Obi is his name, oh my!
O-B-I, O-B-I, O-B-I
Wave to Obi, bye bye bye!
