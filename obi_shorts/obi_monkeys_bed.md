# Five Little Monkeys Jumping on the Bed, with Doctor Obi (about 2:45)

The classic counting rhyme. Five little monkeys brush their teeth, hop into bed and bounce, and one by one they
tumble off onto a soft pile of pillows. Each time Mama Monkey phones the doctor, and Doctor Obi answers from
somewhere funny (his office with a donut, the golf course, a fishing boat, a bubble bath, fast asleep in bed), a
bit of comic relief for the parents. Obi SINGS his line on the beat, as a male voice in the song.

Every little monkey has his own color pajamas with a big number on the top, so kids tie each number to a monkey:
  1 red   2 orange   3 yellow   4 green   5 blue
They fall in order from 5 down to 1, and on each count ("One, two, three, four!") the monkey with that number
jumps, one short shot per number. No numbers are drawn over the picture, so nothing covers a face.

Version 4 structure per verse:
  1. the monkeys still on the bed bouncing (a different camera angle each verse)
  2. the highest number falls: starts on the bed edge, forced to end in the pillows by an [end:] picture
  3. close-up: he rubs his head and CRIES          4. Mama Monkey phones the doctor
  5. Doctor Obi somewhere funny, the red phone rings ("and the doctor said...")
  6. Obi sings "No more monkeys jumping on the bed!" into the phone, lip-synced to the sung line
  7. one quick shot per number on the count, that monkey jumping
The song needs a 10-second instrumental intro for the treehouse, teeth-brushing and running-into-bed shots.
Lyrics are the traditional public-domain rhyme.
