# Five Little Monkeys Jumping on the Bed, with Doctor Obi (about 2:30)

The classic counting rhyme. Five little monkeys bounce on their bed at bedtime, and one by one they tumble off.
Each time, Mama Monkey calls the doctor, and Doctor Obi (with a toy stethoscope and a little white doctor's cap)
answers in a separate spoken male voice. Each verse counts the monkeys still bouncing, with big numbers popping up
on screen. Every tumble lands on a soft pile of pillows, so nobody gets hurt.

The song is sung by a female voice; the doctor's lines are spoken by a male voice and mixed over the song, with the
song turned down under them. Lyrics are the traditional public-domain rhyme.
Title idea: Five Little Monkeys Jumping on the Bed! 🐒🛏️ Count with Doctor Obi | Counting Song for Kids

## Music
Playful silly children's counting song, bouncy and fun, warm friendly female lead vocal with a giggling
children's choir, ukulele, acoustic guitar, bouncy plucked bass, glockenspiel, hand claps, 112 BPM, major key,
leave a short instrumental pause after each "and the doctor said" line, kids music

## Sound effects
boing: a cartoon spring boing sound, playful and bouncy, clean, no music
thud: a soft cartoon pillow bump sound, a gentle poof, clean, no music

## Voices
doctor1: aiden | friendly playful doctor, warm and gently scolding, smiling | No more monkeys jumping on the bed!
doctor2: aiden | friendly playful doctor, a little more firm, smiling | No more monkeys jumping on the bed!
doctor3: aiden | friendly playful doctor, pretending to be very serious | No more monkeys jumping on the bed!
doctor4: aiden | friendly playful doctor, sighing and laughing | No more monkeys jumping on the bed!
doctor5: aiden | friendly doctor, soft and sleepy, gentle | No more monkeys jumping on the bed. Goodnight, little monkeys!
