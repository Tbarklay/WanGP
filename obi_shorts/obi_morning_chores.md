# Old MacDonald: Obi's Morning on the Farm (about 3 minutes)

Morning routine version. The rooster wakes everyone up and Obi helps the farmer with breakfast for every animal:
milk for the calf, eggs from the hens, slop for the pig, hay for the horse, and so on. Each verse is one feeding with
a little mishap. It ends with everyone fed, and the farmer and Obi finally sitting down to their own breakfast.
Gentle and cozy, a good fit for morning playlists.

Title idea: Good Morning, Farm! 🌅🐶 Feeding Time with Obi | Old MacDonald Sing-Along

## Music
Traditional children's nursery rhyme, bright cheerful morning sing-along, warm friendly male vocal with a
children's choir on the E-I-E-I-O, ukulele, acoustic guitar, whistling, glockenspiel, light hand claps, gentle
bouncy rhythm, 110 BPM, major key, kids music
