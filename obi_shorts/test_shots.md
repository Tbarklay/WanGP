# Obi on the Farm: 3-shot test

Each shot is one keyframe (a still picture drawn from the character references) and one motion line
(what happens when that picture is animated into a 5 second clip). `{obi}` and `{farmer}` use the 3D
references in `refs/`. Keep each motion simple: one clear action per shot animates best.

**01** `[keyframe: low angle close-up of {obi} bursting through a gap in a wooden farm fence on a rainy morning, ears flying, tongue out, three surprised black and white cows behind him turning their heads]`
`[motion: the puppy bounds forward toward the camera with a big happy jump, ears flapping, the cows blink and lean back in surprise, gentle camera push in]`

**02** `[keyframe: {farmer} standing beside {truck} with its wooden ramp down, scratching his head, while a chubby black and white cow sits stubbornly in the mud at the bottom of the ramp and {obi} tugs on its tail with his teeth]`
`[motion: the puppy pulls backward on the cow's tail with all his strength, paws slipping in the mud, the cow refuses to move and turns its head to look at him, the farmer laughs]`

**03** `[keyframe: front view through the windshield of {truck} driving down a country road, {farmer} at the wheel smiling, {obi} sitting proudly in the passenger seat wearing a tiny cap, three cows squeezed together in the back looking out the rear window]`
`[motion: the truck bounces gently along the road, the puppy wags his tail and pants happily, the cows sway together in the back, the farmer taps the steering wheel]`
