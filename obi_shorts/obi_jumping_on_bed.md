# Five Little Piglets Jumping on the Bed (about 2:30)

Farm twist on the classic counting rhyme. At bedtime, five little piglets sneak onto the farmer's big patchwork bed
and bounce. One by one they tumble off onto a soft pile of hay, and Obi plays the doctor with a little toy stethoscope.
Each verse counts the piglets still bouncing, with big numbers popping up on screen. Nobody gets hurt: every fall
lands softly in the hay, and every piglet giggles.

Lyrics are our own farm version of the traditional public-domain counting rhyme (piglets instead of monkeys, Obi instead of the doctor).
Title idea: Five Little Piglets Jumping on the Bed! 🐷🛏️ Count with Obi | Counting Song for Kids

## Music
Playful silly children's counting song, bouncy and fun, warm friendly male vocal with a giggling children's choir,
ukulele, acoustic guitar, boing sound-like bass plucks, glockenspiel, hand claps, 116 BPM, major key, kids music

## Sound effects
boing: a cartoon spring boing sound, playful and bouncy, clean, no music
oink: a real little pig oinking and squealing happily, natural clear recording, no music
