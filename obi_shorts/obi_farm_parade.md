# Old MacDonald: Obi's Farm Parade (about 3 minutes)

Our own Old MacDonald video in the animated 3D style. The farmer is getting the barn ready for a party, and Obi
runs around the farm inviting each animal, with a different gag every time. Each animal joins a growing parade
behind Obi, and everyone ends up dancing in the barn.

Structure (from studying successful long farm-song videos): 36 shots of about 5 seconds, one verse per animal,
4 shots per verse so kids can predict the pattern: Obi finds the animal, the animal's sound close-up, a funny
moment together, the animal joins the parade. Close-ups of big expressions land the jokes.

## Music
Traditional children's nursery rhyme, happy upbeat sing-along, warm friendly male vocal with a children's choir
on the E-I-E-I-O, banjo, acoustic guitar, fiddle, hand claps, bouncy country rhythm, 116 BPM, major key, kids music
