# Old MacDonald: Obi Saves the Farm Animals from the Storm (about 3 minutes)

Rainy version. Dark clouds roll over the farm and Obi races around herding every animal into the barn before the
storm, while the farmer holds the big doors open. Each verse is one rescue with its own problem to solve. The storm
peaks with thunder for the last rescue, then clears into a rainbow with everyone dry and cozy in the barn.

Title idea: Obi Saves the Farm Animals from the Storm! ⛈️🐶 Old MacDonald Sing-Along

## Music
Traditional children's nursery rhyme, upbeat sing-along with a playful adventurous feel, warm friendly male vocal
with a children's choir on the E-I-E-I-O, acoustic guitar, banjo, fiddle, light rain-like percussion, hand claps,
bouncy country rhythm, 118 BPM, major key, kids music
