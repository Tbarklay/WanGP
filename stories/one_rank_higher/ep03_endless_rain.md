# One Rank Higher, Episode 3: "The Endless Rain"

Length target: 22-25 minutes (about 3,500 narrated words at a steady storytelling pace). Covers the night of day 6
through day 8. No background music.
Each beat is one illustration, shown while its narration plays. `{yunjae}` and the other names in braces are filled in
with the matching description from `cast.json`, so every image draws the characters the same way.
Lines starting SYSTEM: are read in the System's voice; `[image: same]` keeps the previous picture on screen for them.
`[ui: ...]` after an image draws a status window or ranking board as exact text over the picture. System lines get no
pop-up: her voice carries them.

New this episode: `{fennick}` (the Carpenter card refined to a blue Shipwright) and `{dorran}` (Dorran Shieldwall,
a blue Shield-bearer card refined to purple). Their sheets go in `refs/fennick.png` and `refs/dorran.png`.

## The shape in the dark

**04** `[image: the black ocean at night, six small square plain wooden rafts tied together side by side into one dark floating platform far away, silhouettes of many men standing on it, no lights at all, stars above, ominous]`
Six rafts, lashed together. A dozen men. No torches. Tae-gu learned his lesson. He just sits out there, on the edge of the dark, and watches.

**05** `[image: {yunjae} crouching at the bow of {raft2} at night, holding very still, watching the horizon, {aeliss} kneeling beside him with her staff, tense]`
Yun-jae doesn't light anything. Neither does Aeliss. For almost an hour, two rafts stare at each other across a black ocean.

**06** `[image: close-up of {aeliss} at night with her eyes closed and one hand resting on the water, her pearl glowing faintly, concentrating]`
"Are they coming closer?" he whispers. Aeliss touches the water. "No, master." That's all. She doesn't say more unless he asks.

**07** `[image: same]`

**08** `[image: the dark lashed platform of six plain rafts turning away into the darkness at night, men paddling, small and fading, stars above]`
And they are. The shape turns and slides back into the night. No attack. No shouting. Which is worse.

**09** `[image: close-up of {yunjae} at night frowning, puzzled, the empty dark sea behind him]`
Tae-gu paddled all this way just to look. That only makes sense if he's waiting for something.

**10** `[image: a floating translucent hologram panel made of soft blue light with a small glowing envelope icon, close in front of the dark sea at night, abstract glyphs, no letters, no words, no people, no faces]`
At two in the morning, a private message arrives. From the last person he expected. Gil.

**11** `[image: {gil} hunched alone at the back edge of a plain raft at night, typing on a small floating glowing blue window hidden under his yellow raincoat, glancing over his shoulder nervously]`
"He's not coming tonight. He's waiting for the rain. He read the girl's post in zone chat. He thinks nobody can see anything in the rain. Not even your elf."

**12** `[image: same]`

**13** `[image: close-up of {yunjae} at night reading the message, his face lit blue, thinking hard]`

**14** `[image: {yunjae} at night tapping a floating translucent hologram panel made of soft blue light with one finger, a small focused look, abstract glyphs, no letters]`
But traps want something from you. Gil isn't asking for anything. So he writes back: "When you see a green light, cut. There's a place for you after." Then he closes the window and starts counting.

## Two problems

**15** `[image: {yunjae} sitting cross-legged on {raft2} at night, a few small planks, a coil of rope, a folded tarp and a pot of black pitch laid out in front of him, thinking]`
Problem one. In a day and a half, it starts raining, and it doesn't stop. His raft is three meters of planks with a sail. No roof. Nowhere to go.

**16** `[image: {yunjae} at night looking down at the green carpenter card in his hand, a faint green glow on his face, the dark sea behind him]`
He has a Carpenter card, a tarp and a pot of sealant. That's a start. It's not a shelter.

**17** `[image: {yunjae} at night looking over at {hobb} asleep and snoring on {raft2} with a ladle on his chest, and {aeliss} sitting quietly at the edge, a wry look]`
Problem two. Twelve men are coming in the rain. And his whole army is a cook and an elf who calls fish.

**18** `[image: close-up of {aeliss} at night, calm, her pearl glowing softly, rain clouds gathering in the distant sky behind her]`
Aeliss is strong. But she's a caller, not a wall. If six rafts hit them at once, someone gets past her. And she can't watch every side.

**19** `[image: close-up of {yunjae} at night, eyes sharp, piecing it together, a faint smile]`
He needs builders for the first problem and a shield for the second. And he has one card. So he does what he always does. He looks for a trade.

**20** `[image: a floating translucent hologram panel made of soft blue light with a small glowing envelope icon close in front of a calm sea at dawn, abstract glyphs, no letters, no words, no people]`

**21** `[image: {jiho} on a plain raft like {raft} at dawn, holding up a glowing blue citizen card with a shield silhouette on it, nervous and excited]`

**22** `[image: close-up of a glowing blue citizen card with the silhouette of a warrior holding a huge steel shield, held in a thin nervous hand, dawn light]`

## The trade

And Ji-ho can't use it. A citizen has to eat. A soldier eats a lot. And Ji-ho still catches one fish a day, if he's lucky.

**27** `[image: {jiho} on his plain raft at dawn surrounded by fish and water jugs appearing in bursts of blue light, his mouth open in shock]`

**28** `[image: {jiho} at dawn grinning and wiping his eyes with his sleeve, holding a jug of water, on his small plain raft]`

**30** `[image: {hobb} waking up on {raft2} in the morning, rubbing his eyes and staring at a much smaller pile of fish, shocked and offended, holding a ladle]`

## The Carpenter

**32** `[image: {yunjae} on {raft2} in bright morning light holding the green carpenter card, blue light gathering under his palm, focused]`
Day six. Three fresh Refines. The first one goes to the card he's been saving.

**33** `[image: close-up of the green carpenter card glowing brighter, its frame turning a deep sapphire blue, sparkles, the morning sea behind]`
Green. Then blue. Carpenter becomes Shipwright. A builder who doesn't just nail planks together. He builds things that float.

**34** `[image: a pillar of soft blue light rising from {raft2} in the morning, a man's silhouette forming inside it, {aeliss} and {hobb} watching with wide eyes]`
He activates it. The light rises, folds, and becomes a man.

**35** `[image: {fennick} standing on {raft2} in morning light, blinking at the open ocean, running his hand along the raft's planks with a critical frown]`
A wiry, sunburned man with a red-brown beard and a pencil behind one ear. The first thing he does isn't bow. It's kneel down and knock on the planks.

**36** `[image: close-up of {fennick} in morning light frowning at a rope knot on the raft, unimpressed]`
"Who tied this?" Yun-jae says he did. Fennick sighs like a man who has seen a lot of bad knots. "Then you're lucky it's floating."

**37** `[image: {raft2} seen from high above in bright morning light, small in the lower part of the frame on the sparkling open sea, a carpenter's mallet and a coil of rope on the deck, a large soft glowing blue light hovering in the empty air above it, wide shot, no people visible]` `[ui: status | Fennick | Shipwright of Corrow Bay · RARE | HP 62/100 | STR 40/100 | AGI 44/100 | MANA 28/100 | Loyalty 61%]`
Fennick. Shipwright of Corrow Bay. Rare. Health sixty-two. Strength forty. Agility forty-four. Mana twenty-eight. Loyalty sixty-one percent.

**38** `[image: {fennick} sitting on {raft2} in the morning with his chin in his hand, staring sadly out at the open ocean, quiet]`
Corrow Bay was a harbor town. Its world lasted forty-two days. He built boats for everyone there. None of them were enough.

**39** `[image: {yunjae} crouching beside {fennick} on {raft2} in morning light, explaining with his hands, Fennick listening seriously]`
Yun-jae tells him about the riddle. Rain that never stops. All that lies low will drink until it drowns.

**40** `[image: close-up of {fennick} in morning light, a slow grin spreading under his beard, eyes bright, rolling up his sleeves]`
Fennick listens. Then he grins for the first time. "So you don't need a raft. You need a roof that floats. Get me wood."

## The shelter

**41** `[image: {yunjae} on {raft2} in morning light holding up a large pile of fish to a floating translucent golden hologram panel, the fish dissolving into glowing gold coins, {aeliss} calling more fish at the edge]`
Wood costs two gold a plank. So all morning, Aeliss calls fish, Hobb cleans them, and Yun-jae sells them faster than the zone can eat.

**42** `[image: a big stack of fresh wooden planks, thick logs, coils of rope and a box of iron nails appearing on the deck of {raft2} in a burst of golden light, late morning]`
By noon he has forty planks, eight logs, three coils of rope and a box of nails. Every coin he has. Spent on the plainest things in the shop.

**43** `[image: {fennick} working fast on {raft2} at midday, lashing thick logs into a large square frame around the small raft, sawdust flying, his hands a blur, focused]`
And Fennick goes to work. He doesn't build like a man. He builds like a man who has done this ten thousand times and is mildly annoyed by every one of them.

**44** `[image: {fennick} and {yunjae} on a half-built log frame on the sea at midday, Fennick pointing at the joints and explaining, Yun-jae hammering a peg, both sweaty]`
Logs under the planks, to lift the deck higher. A wider frame, four meters. "Low drinks," Fennick says. "So we don't sit low."

**45** `[image: {fennick} carving a long sloped channel along the edge of a half-built wooden deck on the sea in afternoon light, showing {yunjae} how it slopes outward]`
Then he cuts channels into the deck, sloping outward, so every drop that lands on it runs off into the sea instead of pooling.

**46** `[image: {yunjae} on a half-built log raft in afternoon light watching {fennick} work, impressed, a small smile, the repairman in him delighted]`
Yun-jae has fixed machines all his life. He's never watched someone this good with their hands. He just watches, and learns, and passes him nails.

**47** `[image: {hobb} in afternoon light on a half-built log raft talking cheerfully to {fennick} with a wooden spoon raised, Fennick ignoring him and hammering]`
Hobb tries to talk to the new man all afternoon. Fennick answers in hammer strokes. Hobb decides they're best friends too.

**48** `[image: {yunjae} on a half-built log raft at sunset holding the pot of black pitch with blue light gathering under his palm, focused]`
At sunset, the second Refine. The pot of sealant. White to green.

**49** `[image: close-up of a pot of black pitch glowing green, the pitch inside moving by itself like slow liquid, sparkles, sunset light]`
Everseal. It doesn't need brushing. Pour it on a seam, and it finds every crack by itself and closes it.

**50** `[image: {fennick} pouring glowing green-tinted black pitch along the seams of a wooden roof frame at sunset, the pitch flowing into the cracks by itself, delighted and amazed]`
Fennick pours one drop on a seam and watches it crawl along the joint and seal it shut. For the first time all day, he stops working. "I want one of those," he says quietly. "Back home, I wanted one of those my whole life."

**51** `[image: {fennick} and {yunjae} raising a sloped wooden roof frame over the middle of a large log raft at dusk, ropes taut, the sea calm and orange]`
They work through the night. A sloped roof over the middle of the raft. Two barrels Fennick builds from spare planks, with cloth funnels to catch the rain. And a small iron stove, the last thing in the shop he could afford, under cover in the middle.

**52** `[image: {yunjae} at dawn on a large log raft holding the folded waterproof tarp, blue light gathering under his palm, tired but determined]`
Day seven. Dawn. Fresh Refines. The first one goes to the tarp.

**53** `[image: a waterproof tarp unfolding by itself in the dawn light, turning from plain gray to a deep storm-blue fabric with a faint silver shimmer, sparkles]`
Plain tarp. Then green. Stormcloth. Light as a sail, tough as leather, and no water gets through it. Not even a drop.

**54** `[image: {raft3} at dawn on a calm sea, brand new, a storm-blue fabric stretched tight over its wooden roof shelter, wide shot, no people, rain clouds gathering on the horizon]`
Fennick stretches it over the roof and pins it down. And when he steps back, the raft isn't a raft anymore.

**55** `[image: {raft3} seen from high above at dawn, small in the lower part of the frame on the calm open sea, a large soft glowing green light hovering in the empty air above it, wide shot, no people visible]`
SYSTEM: Raft upgraded. Level three. Shelter Raft. Congratulations. You now own a floating hut.

**56** `[image: close-up of the glowing green plank set into the new wooden deck of a larger log raft at dawn, new planks all around it, dew on the wood]`
And right in the middle of the new deck, Fennick kept the first plank. The green one. "It's the only good plank you had," he says. "You don't throw away the good one."

**57** `[image: {yunjae} standing under the roof of {raft3} at dawn, looking up at the storm-blue fabric with quiet pride, {fennick} wiping his hands on a rag beside him, tired]`
Yun-jae doesn't say anything for a while. Then he thanks him. Fennick shrugs. But his Loyalty ticks up three points, and he pretends not to notice.

## The shield

**58** `[image: {yunjae} sitting under the roof of {raft3} in the morning holding the glowing blue shield-bearer card, the dark sky building on the horizon]`
Five hours until noon. One problem solved. And one blue card left to spend his second Refine on.

**59** `[image: close-up of the blue shield-bearer card glowing, its frame turning a rich royal purple, purple sparks, morning light]`
Blue. Then purple. Rare becomes Epic.

**60** `[image: a tall pillar of purple light rising from {raft3} in the morning, shaking the water around the raft, a huge armored silhouette forming inside it, {aeliss} shielding her eyes]`
He activates it. The light comes up purple and heavy, and the whole raft dips under the weight of what's standing in it.

**61** `[image: {dorran} standing tall on {raft3} in the morning after the light fades, his huge steel shield planted on the deck, looking calmly at the open ocean, massive and still]`
The man who steps out of it is huge. Gray beard. A scar across his nose. Armor dented by a hundred battles. A tower shield nearly as tall as he is.

**62** `[image: {dorran} looking slowly around the open ocean in the morning, then down at {yunjae}, calm and serious, no fear on his face]`
He looks at the ocean. Then at the sky. Then at Yun-jae. And he asks one question. "Where is the wall, Commander?"

**63** `[image: {yunjae} standing in front of {dorran} on {raft3} in the morning, gesturing at the raised edges of the raft, Dorran listening]`

**64** `[image: {raft3} seen from high above in the morning, small in the lower part of the frame on the gray open sea, a huge steel steel shield lying on the deck, a large soft glowing purple light hovering in the empty air above it, wide shot, no people visible]` `[ui: status | Dorran Shieldwall | The Last Wall of Harrowmark · EPIC | HP 98/100 | STR 74/100 | AGI 20/100 | MANA 12/100 | Loyalty 72%]`
Dorran Shieldwall. The Last Wall of Harrowmark. Epic. Health ninety-eight. Strength seventy-four. Agility twenty. Mana twelve. Loyalty seventy-two percent, the highest any citizen has ever started at.

**65** `[image: close-up of {dorran} in the morning, his scarred face calm and tired, rain clouds behind him]`
Harrowmark was a kingdom. When its world fell, he was holding its gate. He held it for nine hours. When it was over, he was the only thing still standing.

**66** `[image: same]`
SYSTEM: This citizen has never once retreated. The System would like to point out that this is not always a good thing.

**67** `[image: {dorran} walking around the edge of {raft3} in the morning, testing the raised edges with his hand, studying it like a battlefield, {yunjae} watching]`
Dorran walks the edge of the raft once. Slowly. Like he's walking the walls of a castle. "Four sides. Raised edge. Only the stern is low. They'll come at the stern."

**68** `[image: close-up of {yunjae} in the morning looking at Dorran with surprise and a slow smile]`
Yun-jae had worked that out an hour ago. Dorran worked it out in a minute.

**69** `[image: {hobb} holding out a bowl of glowing stew to {dorran} on {raft3} in the morning, chattering away, Dorran taking it and nodding once without a word]`
Hobb brings him a bowl of stew and talks at him for ten minutes. Dorran says one word. "Good." Hobb now has three silent best friends, and is the happiest man on the ocean.

## Noon

**70** `[image: a wide view of the ocean at midday under a huge black wall of storm clouds rolling in, the sea turning dark gray, tiny plain rafts scattered across it, ominous]`
And then it's noon. The sky over the whole zone goes the color of a bruise.

**71** `[image: same]`
SYSTEM: Attention, candidates. The first World Event has begun. The Endless Rain. Duration: twenty-four hours. Good luck. You'll need roofs more than luck.

**72** `[image: the first huge raindrops hitting the dark ocean, each one making a ring, the surface starting to churn, close-up, moody]`
The first drops are heavy. The tenth are heavier. By the hundredth, the sea is boiling with them.

**73** `[image: a floating translucent golden hologram panel at midday with a small hourglass icon cracking and fading away, rain falling through it, abstract, no letters, no words]`
And in the corner of the shop, the little hourglass runs out. The limited section closes. Tarps, barrels, sealant, buckets. Gone. Exactly as the event begins.

**74** `[image: close-up of {yunjae} under the roof of {raft3} watching the hourglass vanish, rain streaming off the roof edge in front of him, eyes narrowing]`
Yun-jae watches it go. Not a coincidence, then. The shop told everyone what was coming. It just didn't say it out loud.

**75** `[image: survivors on plain rafts in pouring rain, desperately bailing water out with their hands and shoes, panicked, their rafts riding low in the water, gray daylight]`
Within an hour, zone chat is screaming. Rafts that were dry at noon are ankle deep at one. People bail with their hands. With their shoes.

**76** `[image: a crowd of small floating translucent blue hologram panels over the rainy ocean, each with a tiny bucket icon and stacks of gold coin icons, abstract, no letters, no words]`
And then the offers start. Forty gold for a tin bucket. A bucket that cost two an hour ago. Sixty for a tarp. A hundred for anything with a roof.

**77** `[image: close-up of {yunjae} under the roof of {raft3}, thoughtful, rain pouring off the edge of the roof, counting on his fingers]`
Yun-jae does the math. A tin bucket is a tin bucket. But the moment the section closes, it's worth twenty times as much. And nobody can buy one anywhere.

**78** `[image: {yunjae} under the roof of {raft3} in the rain, closing a floating golden window with a quiet, determined look, filing something away]`
He doesn't sell anything. He has nothing spare to sell. But he remembers it. Next time, he won't buy one tarp. He'll buy wood and cloth for a hundred.

**79** `[image: {aeliss} standing at the edge of {raft3} in heavy rain with her staff raised, a smooth current of water pouring off the deck back into the sea, her hair soaked, calm]`
On his own raft, nobody is bailing. Fennick's channels carry the water off the deck. The roof funnels it into the barrels. And when it comes in too fast, Aeliss lifts her staff and the water just leaves.

**80** `[image: {hobb} under the roof of {raft3} stirring a big steaming glowing pot on the small iron stove, warm orange light, rain pouring outside, cozy and happy]`
Hobb's stove stays lit. His soup glows. Eat it, and the cold stays out of your bones for a few hours.

**81** `[image: {fennick} under the roof of {raft3} checking the seams of the roof with his fingers, rain drumming on the fabric above, satisfied]`
Fennick walks the roof every hour, checking seams that don't need checking. Every one holds. He doesn't smile. But he walks a little taller each time.

**82** `[image: {dorran} standing at the low stern of {raft3} in pouring rain, his steel shield planted, motionless, rain running off his armor, staring into the gray]`
And Dorran stands at the stern. In the rain. He doesn't eat. He doesn't sit. He just stands there. Hour after hour.

## No torches

**93** `[image: the black stormy ocean at night lit by a flash of lightning, the dark platform of six plain rafts lashed together riding very low in the water, half sunk, a dozen soaked men on it paddling hard, sheets of rain, ominous]`
Gil was right. They came in the rain. No torches. In this storm you can't see two meters. But Aeliss doesn't need to see. She feels every paddle stroke through the water.

**94** `[image: close-up of {taegu} at night in pouring rain, soaked, gritting his teeth, his skin with a faint metallic sheen, lit by lightning, furious and desperate]`
And Tae-gu's platform is in trouble. Six rafts with no roof, filling for eleven hours. They've been bailing since noon. They're cold, exhausted, and riding so low the waves wash over their feet.

**95** `[image: close-up of {yunjae} at night under the roof, calm, rain pouring off the roof edge, lightning in his eyes]`
All that lies low will drink until it drowns. Tae-gu didn't come here because he's strong. He came because he's sinking. And a man who's sinking doesn't stop.

**96** `[image: {yunjae} under the roof of {raft3} at night giving quick quiet orders, {dorran} and {aeliss} listening, rain behind them, lit by the stove]`
Yun-jae gives three orders. Dorran, the stern. Aeliss, the water. Hobb and Fennick, under the roof, and don't come out. And then he covers the green plank with his jacket.

**97** `[image: {dorran} at night at the low stern of {raft3} in pouring rain, raising his huge steel shield and planting his feet, calm, lightning behind him]`
Dorran doesn't move from the stern. He's been standing there since noon. He can stand there until morning.

**98** `[image: the dark platform of six plain rafts crashing into the back of {raft3} at night in pouring rain, lit by lightning, soaked men leaping across with clubs and knives, chaos]`

**99** `[image: {dorran} at night slamming his huge steel shield into two soaked raiders trying to climb onto {raft3}, knocking them back into the water, rain flying, lightning]`

The next three hit the shield together. Clubs. A knife. A hook. Dorran doesn't step back. He doesn't even grunt. It's like hitting a door.

**101** `[image: soaked raiders at night trying to climb the high raised side edges of {raft3} and slipping off the wet wood, rain pouring, lightning]`

**103** `[image: {aeliss} at night at the side of {raft3} in pouring rain, her staff raised high, her pearl blazing aqua, the sea beneath the raider platform starting to heave and lift, dramatic]`
"Now," says Yun-jae. And Aeliss raises her staff.

**104** `[image: the dark platform of six plain rafts at night tilting sharply as a surge of water lifts one side, rainwater sloshing across the decks, men falling and grabbing for ropes, lightning]`

**105** `[image: close-up of thick tan ropes lashing two plain wooden rafts together at night, straining and stretching tight under the weight, rain pouring, about to snap]`

**106** `[image: {yunjae} at night pulling his jacket off the deck of {raft3}, revealing the glowing green plank, its bright green light shining out through the rain]`

**107** `[image: {gil} at night at the back of the dark raider platform in pouring rain, seeing a green light in the distance, his face lit green, then raising his hook to the rope, determined]`

**108** `[image: the raider platform at night splitting apart, the lashing ropes snapping and whipping through the air, six plain rafts breaking away from each other, men falling into the water, lightning, chaos]`
The first rope goes. The weight shifts. The second snaps on its own. Then the third. Then the whole platform tears apart like wet paper.

**109** `[image: six small separate plain rafts spinning away from each other in the stormy sea at night, men clinging to them and bailing, lightning, rain]`
Six rafts. Spinning away from each other in the dark. Each one low, heavy, and half full of water.

**110** `[image: {taegu} at night with iron-gray metallic skin leaping from a tilting plain raft toward the stern of {raft3}, arms outstretched, furious, rain, lightning]`
And Tae-gu, standing on the middle raft as it tips under his iron weight, does the only thing he can. He jumps. Straight for the stern.

**111** `[image: {dorran} at night swinging his huge steel shield to meet {taegu} in mid-air at the stern of {raft3}, a massive impact, rain exploding off the steel, lightning]`
Straight at the wall.

**113** `[image: a dark shape of a large man sinking straight down into the black water underneath the rain-beaten surface, a trail of bubbles rising, moody, underwater view]`
Iron Body. Nothing can cut him. Nothing can break him. And nothing that heavy can float.

**114** `[image: soaked raiders at night on a plain raft frantically throwing ropes into the water and hauling, shouting, rain pouring, lightning]`
For the second time in a week, Bang Tae-gu sinks like a stone. His men forget the raft. They forget the elf. They throw every rope they have into the water and pull.

**115** `[image: {taegu} at night being hauled half-drowned onto a small sinking plain raft by his soaked men, coughing up water, his skin back to normal, rain pouring]`

**116** `[image: {dorran} at night at the stern of {raft3}, his steel shield lowered, looking calmly down at the beaten raiders in the water, rain running off him, lightning]`
Dorran hasn't moved. He's not even breathing hard.

**117** `[image: close-up of {yunjae} at night at the edge of the roof, rain pouring between him and the dark sea, his face calm and cold]`
Yun-jae could end it here. Aeliss could sink every one of those rafts with a wave. And under the loot rule, everything they own would be his.

## The map

**136** `[image: {yunjae} on {raft3} at sunset holding a small blue wooden chest with blue light gathering under his palms, {aeliss} and {dorran} watching]`
