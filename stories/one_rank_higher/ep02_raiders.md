# One Rank Higher, Episode 2: "Raiders"

Length target: 23-26 minutes (about 3,600 narrated words at a steady storytelling pace). Covers the night of day 2
through day 6. No background music.
Each beat is one illustration, shown while its narration plays. `{yunjae}` and the other names in braces are filled in
with the matching description from `cast.json`, so every image draws the characters the same way.
Lines starting SYSTEM: are read in the System's voice; `[image: same]` keeps the previous picture on screen for them.
`[ui: ...]` after an image draws a status window or ranking board as exact text over the picture. System lines get no
pop-up: her voice carries them.

## The repairman thinks

**17** `[image: {yunjae} sitting very still on {raft2} at night, one hand on his chin, deep in thought, the three torches reflected in the water]`
Most people would panic. Yun-jae does what he's done his whole life when something won't work. He stops looking at the problem, and starts looking at the parts.

**18** `[image: a diagram-like view of three small torch-lit rafts on dark water from above, glowing torches, abstract, no text]`
Three men he can't fight. Three rafts made of four planks and some rope. And three torches, burning bright in the middle of the night.

**19** `[image: flashback, the gigantic sea monster with glowing white eyes circling a faintly glowing raft at night, moonlight on the water]`
Last night, something longer than a bus circled his raft for an hour. It wasn't hunting him. It was hunting the light.

**20** `[image: close-up of {yunjae}'s face at night, a slow cold realization, a faint smile]`
He looks at the torches again. And for the first time since they appeared, he relaxes.

**21** `[image: {yunjae} leaning close to {aeliss} on {raft2} at night, whispering, her face calm, her pearl glowing faintly]`
He leans over to Aeliss and whispers. "That thing from last night. Is it still close?" She closes her eyes for a moment. "Yes, master. Below us. Waiting."

**22** `[image: {yunjae} quickly pulling the patched sail cloth down over the glowing green plank on {raft2} at night, the raft going dark]`

**23** `[image: the three raider rafts at night, their torches now the only lights on the entire black ocean, glowing like beacons]`
Now there are only three lights on the whole ocean. And all of them belong to the men who came to rob him.

## The deal

**24** `[image: {taegu} at night squinting into the darkness where the raft disappeared, holding his torch higher, confused and annoyed]`
Tae-gu squints into the dark. "Hey! Hiding won't help. We can hear you breathing over there."

**25** `[image: {gil} swinging his rope and metal hook over his head at night, about to throw it toward the darkness, torch in his teeth]`
Gil starts swinging his hook. One throw, and he'll catch the edge of the raft and pull them close.

**26** `[image: a metal hook on a rope flying through the dark and catching the raised edge of {raft2} with a clank, the rope pulling tight, night]`
The hook flies out of the dark and bites into the edge of the raft with a clank. The rope goes tight. Slowly, the raider rafts start pulling them in.

**27** `[image: {aeliss} at night looking calmly at the taut rope, her staff lowered, waiting, {yunjae} beside her with one hand raised to stop her]`
Aeliss lifts her staff. Yun-jae puts a hand on her arm. Not yet. Fighting them is the one thing he can't afford to do.

Instead, he works the hook loose with two fingers, the way he'd free a stuck cable from a washing machine, and lets it drop into the sea. Gil reels in an empty rope and stares at it.

**29** `[image: {yunjae} calling out across the black water at night, hands cupped, calm and steady, {aeliss} crouching behind him]`

**30** `[image: {yunjae} at night tying a leather bait pouch and a few fish to a loose plank, working fast, determined]`
He ties his bait pouch and the rest of today's fish to a spare plank. The same trick as last night. Except this time, the decoy isn't for the monster.

**31** `[image: {yunjae} and {aeliss} kneeling together at the edge of {raft2} in the dark, his hand over the bait plank, her staff touching the water]`
He puts his hand on the plank. One Refine left. He spends it. Not on a weapon. On bait.

**32** `[image: close-up of a plain bait pouch on a plank glowing with soft green light, its leather turning rich and sleek, a strong glittering scent cloud rising into the water, magical]`
"Refine." The pouch glows green. Common bait, to Uncommon. And in the water around it, a glittering cloud spreads out, the kind of smell that carries for kilometers.

**34** `[image: {aeliss} at night touching her staff to the water, a narrow glowing current carrying the dark bait plank away from {raft2} toward the torch-lit rafts]`
Aeliss touches her staff to the water, and a quiet current carries the plank away. Not out to sea. Straight toward the three torches.

## Teeth in the dark

**35** `[image: the bait plank bumping gently against the side of {pyo}'s torch-lit raft at night, {pyo} looking down at it, puzzled]`
The plank bumps against Pyo's raft. He looks down at it. Fish. A pouch. "That's it? That's what made the light?"

**36** `[image: the dark water beneath the torch-lit rafts at night, a huge shadow rising from the deep toward the light, a ring of bubbles]`
Under the water, something enormous turns toward the brightest light on the ocean. And now it smells dinner, too.

**37** `[image: {taegu} at night suddenly looking down at the water, his grin fading, the torch shaking in his hand]`
Tae-gu feels it first. The water under his raft rises, just a little. Like the whole ocean took a breath.

**38** `[image: the gigantic sea monster bursting up out of the water beneath {pyo}'s raft at night, jaws wide open, the raft and torch flying into the air, huge splash, terrifying]`
Then Pyo's raft explodes upward.

**39** `[image: the gigantic sea monster crashing back into the black water at night with broken planks raining down, a torch spinning through the air, dramatic, no clear people]`
The monster comes down on it with its whole weight. Planks, rope and a burning torch go flying. And then the water closes, and there's nothing left but splinters.

**40** `[image: {gil} at night screaming and paddling his raft away as fast as he can with both hands, his torch thrown into the sea behind him]`
Gil throws his torch as far as he can and paddles for his life. Smart kid.

**41** `[image: {taegu} at night knocked off his feet as the sea monster rams his raft, clinging to the planks with both hands, water crashing over him]`
The monster rams Tae-gu's raft next. It tips him into the sea.

**42** `[image: underwater at night, {taegu} sinking like a stone, his metal-shining body pulling him down, bubbles streaming up, panicked eyes]`
And here's the thing nobody tells you about having a body made of iron. Iron doesn't float.

**43** `[image: {taegu} at night clawing his way back onto the edge of {gil}'s raft, gasping, Gil pulling him by the arm with both hands, terrified]`
He sinks like an anchor. Only Gil's rope saves him. The kid drags him onto his raft, coughing up seawater, and they paddle into the dark without looking back.

**44** `[image: {taegu} lying on {gil}'s raft at night, soaking wet and coughing, staring up at the stars with murderous eyes, {gil} paddling frantically behind him]`
On Gil's raft, Tae-gu lies on his back, coughing up the ocean. For the first time since the Summoning, Iron Body didn't protect him. It nearly killed him.

**45** `[image: close-up of {taegu}'s face at night, wet, furious, eyes burning, teeth clenched]`
He doesn't feel lucky. He feels humiliated. Somewhere in that darkness is a man who beat him without ever showing his face. And Tae-gu has never been good at letting things go.

**46** `[image: the black ocean at night, calm again, a few floating splinters and a dying torch drifting on the water, somber]`
And then it's quiet. The monster sinks back into the deep. The last torch floats on the water for a while, and goes out.

Yun-jae stares at the cards. A few minutes ago, they belonged to a man who was alive.

**57** `[image: {yunjae} nodding slowly at night, jaw tight, looking out at the dark water, {aeliss} beside him]`
He nods. He doesn't feel better. But he understands. Out here, being kind and being careful are going to have to be the same thing.

Later, when he opens her status window, her mana is lower from tonight. But her loyalty has jumped to sixty-eight. And the pink bar has moved, too. Nine percent.

Yun-jae doesn't answer. Not yet. A kid who switches sides once can switch sides twice. But he doesn't block him, either. In the Drift, every door you leave open is a door you might need later.

## The Cook

**72** `[image: close-up of a hand holding a glowing white citizen card with a plump cook silhouette holding a ladle, soft white glow, the morning sea behind]`
Instead, he takes out the card he traded for on day one. The Cook. Common.

**73** `[image: {aeliss} kneeling at the edge of {raft2} in morning light, her staff in the water, a big silver school of fish circling the raft]`
On day one, he couldn't afford another mouth. Now Aeliss calls more fish every morning than the two of them can eat. That changes the math.

**74** `[image: {yunjae} holding the white cook card with blue light gathering under his palm, focused, morning sun]`
Three fresh Refines. He spends the first one on the card.

**75** `[image: the white cook card glowing and its frame turning green, sparkles, the morning sea behind]`
"Refine." The white frame turns green. Uncommon. No luck this time. He'll take it.

**76** `[image: a soft pillar of green light rising from {raft2} in the morning, {aeliss} watching it]`
He activates it. Green light rises off the raft. Much smaller than Aeliss's pillar. Much less likely to bring anybody paddling.

**77** `[image: {hobb} standing on {raft2} as green light fades around him, blinking in surprise, holding a wooden ladle, the morning ocean behind him]`
When it fades, a short, round, bearded man is standing on the raft, holding a ladle. He looks at the ocean. He looks at Yun-jae. He looks at Aeliss.

**78** `[image: {hobb} grinning widely and talking with big hand gestures on {raft2}, {yunjae} looking slightly overwhelmed, {aeliss} expressionless behind]`
Then he talks. And talks. "Name's Hobb, lad! Cook, forty years, two kitchens and a war! Is that a fish? Is that an elf? Where's the kitchen? Don't tell me there's no kitchen."

**79** `[image: {yunjae} raising his hand toward {hobb}, a glowing blue status window opening beside him, abstract glowing bars, no readable text]` `[ui: status | Hobb | Hearth Cook · UNCOMMON | HP 85/100 | STR 38/100 | AGI 18/100 | MANA 24/100 | Loyalty 64%]`
His status window says Hearth Cook. Uncommon. No affinity bar. And a loyalty of sixty-four, which, the System notes, is unusually high for someone who just met you.

**80** `[image: same]`
SYSTEM: Some citizens are simply glad to be alive again. Feed them, and they stay that way.

**81** `[image: {hobb} cooking fish on a small iron pan over a little fire in a clay pot on {raft2}, steam and a delicious golden glow rising from the food, happy]`
Within an hour, Hobb has turned a clay pot into a stove and three fish into the best meal Yun-jae has eaten since Seoul.

**82** `[image: close-up of a steaming bowl of fish stew glowing with faint golden light, sparkles, delicious]`
And it's not just good. Hobb's food glows. Eat it, and for a few hours, you're a little stronger, a little faster, a little warmer.

**83** `[image: {aeliss} eating from a bowl on {raft2} with a tiny surprised look, {hobb} watching her proudly, {yunjae} smiling]`
Even Aeliss stops after the first bite, and looks down at the bowl like it surprised her. Hobb takes that as the highest compliment of his life.

**84** `[image: {hobb} pointing his ladle at {aeliss} and chatting cheerfully while she sits silently, {yunjae} trying not to laugh]`
Hobb talks to Aeliss all afternoon. Aeliss says four words back, total. Hobb decides they're best friends.

**85** `[image: {hobb} sitting at the edge of {raft2} at sunset with his ladle across his knees, looking at the sea, his cheerful face gone quiet]`
At sunset, Hobb finally goes quiet. Yun-jae sits down next to him and asks the question he asks every citizen. Where are you from?

**86** `[image: a cozy fantasy harbor town at dusk with warm lantern-lit inns, cobblestone streets and fishing boats, peaceful, nostalgic]`
"Little harbor town called Brindle," Hobb says. "Best inn on the coast. Mine." He smiles. "We held out a long time, lad. Fifty-six days."

**87** `[image: close-up of {yunjae} at sunset, surprised, thinking hard]`
Fifty-six. Aeliss's world fell on day twenty-eight. Hobb's lasted twice as long. So the worlds don't all fall at the first battle. Some of them win one. Maybe two.

**88** `[image: close-up of {hobb} at sunset with a sad smile, rubbing his gray beard]`
"What happened on day fifty-six?" Yun-jae asks. Hobb pats his shoulder and stands up. "Ask me when you've won one, lad. Then it'll be useful." Then he goes back to his stove, humming.

**89** `[image: {aeliss} glancing at {hobb} with a flicker of curiosity at dusk, the first real expression on her face all day]`
Aeliss watches him go. It's the first time all day she's looked interested in anything he said.

## The Carpenter

**95** `[image: a glowing blue trade window floating over the ocean, a white fisherman card dissolving into light, abstract, no readable text]`

**98** `[image: close-up of a glowing private chat window floating in front of {yunjae} at dusk, abstract symbols, no readable text]`

**99** `[image: close-up of {yunjae} at dusk, reading the message, serious, the sun setting behind him]`

## Building, again

**100** `[image: {yunjae} sawing a plank on {raft2} with tools from the builder's kit spread around him, {hobb} holding the other end, morning light]`
Day four. Yun-jae does what he always does when he's worried. He works.

**101** `[image: {yunjae} and {hobb} hammering planks along the edge of {raft2} to raise its sides, {aeliss} watching the horizon, bright morning]`
Pyo's six planks become a raised edge around the raft. Waist high. Hard to climb. Hard to board.

**102** `[image: close-up of a plain kitchen knife glowing with green light on the deck, turning sleek and sharp, sparkles]`
He refines the kitchen knife. White to green. Keen Knife. It's the first thing he's owned that could count as a weapon. He hopes he never has to use it.

**103** `[image: {yunjae} tying a net made of rope beneath the edge of {raft2}, leaning over the water, focused, afternoon light]`
And under the raft, out of sight, he strings Pyo's rope into a net. It catches fish while they sail. It also catches anything that tries to swim up underneath.

**104** `[image: {hobb} setting up a small proper stove made from a clay pot and stones on {raft2}, a pot bubbling, proud, {yunjae} smiling]`
Hobb gets a real stove. Mostly so he'll stop asking.

**105** `[image: {hobb} proudly presenting a steaming bowl to {aeliss} on {raft2}, {aeliss} taking it with a perfectly blank face, {yunjae} laughing in the background]`
He celebrates by cooking Aeliss a special fish stew, and asking her opinion. She eats all of it, hands back the bowl, and says, "Acceptable." Hobb tells that story for the rest of the week.

**106** `[image: close-up of a rope fishing net glowing with soft green light under the raft, fish sparkling inside it, the water clear and turquoise]`
The last Refine of day four goes on the net. White to green. Tide Net. It fills itself while they sail.

**107** `[image: {hobb} cooking at his stove on {raft2} with a pile of fresh fish beside him, {yunjae} counting supplies, bright day]`
By day five, they have more food than they can eat, water that never runs out, and a cook who makes both glow. On an ocean where most people are counting their last crackers, the raft with the sail has become the richest place in the zone.

**108** `[image: close-up of {yunjae} looking over his full raft with a worried frown, the sea glittering behind him]`
Which, Yun-jae knows, is exactly what makes it a target.

## The drifter

**109** `[image: a single plain raft like {raft} drifting toward {raft2} in hot afternoon sun, a thin exhausted man lying on it waving one weak hand, the ocean glaring]`
That afternoon, a raft drifts toward them in the heat. One man on it, thin and sunburned, waving weakly. "Water," he croaks. "Please. Two days without water."

**110** `[image: {hobb} reaching for a water flask with a worried face, {yunjae} raising a hand to stop him, studying the stranger carefully]`
Hobb is already reaching for the flask. Yun-jae stops him, gently. Not because he won't help. Because something about this man is bothering him.

**111** `[image: close-up of thick neat rope lashings on the stranger's plain raft, tied with strong practiced knots, sunlight]`
He looks at the stranger's raft. The planks are lashed with thick, fresh rope, tied with the same tight knots he saw on Tae-gu's rafts. A starving drifter with two days of thirst doesn't have new rope.

**112** `[image: close-up of the stranger's eyes flicking past {yunjae} to look at the supplies on the raft, sly, counting]`
And the man's eyes aren't on the water flask. They're on everything else. The stove. The crates. The sail. Counting.

**113** `[image: {yunjae} handing a cup of water and a wrapped fish across to the thin stranger with a friendly face, both rafts side by side in the sun]`
Yun-jae smiles, and gives him water anyway. And a fish. And, very casually, mentions that they're sailing south-east tomorrow, toward the warmer water.

**114** `[image: the stranger's raft drifting away to the west in the afternoon sun, the man paddling much faster than a starving man should, small in the distance]`
The man thanks him and drifts away. West. Straight toward where Ji-ho said Tae-gu's rafts are waiting. Paddling a lot faster than a man who hasn't had water in two days.

**115** `[image: {yunjae} at the tiller of {raft2} quietly turning the sail to head north-west, {aeliss} watching him, the afternoon sun low]`
The next morning, Yun-jae sails north-west. Hobb asks why. "Because a spy is only useful," he says, "if you tell him something."

**116** `[image: {aeliss} looking at {yunjae} with the faintest hint of a smile, wind in her silver hair, the sea bright behind her]`
Aeliss doesn't say anything. But for a second, the corner of her mouth moves. It might be the first time he's seen her almost smile.

## Day five

**117** `[image: close-up of {panel} glowing in bright daylight over the ocean, abstract symbols, no readable text]`
Day five. Around noon, the System sends a message to everyone in the Drift at once.

SYSTEM: Attention, candidates. The first World Event begins in forty-eight hours. A hint will be delivered in twenty-four.

**119** `[image: hundreds of glowing blue chat windows exploding into the sky over the ocean, abstract symbols, no readable text]`
Zone chat loses its mind. Nobody knows what a World Event is. Everybody has a theory. A war. A flood. Dragons.

In world chat, Kaiser posts again. His company now has twenty million members. He says they'll share whatever they learn about the event. Anyone who registers gets the information first.

**121** `[image: close-up of {yunjae} reading world chat with narrowed eyes, the blue glow on his face, the sea behind him]`
Yun-jae notices what that really means. Twenty million people, all reporting to one man. Kaiser doesn't need to explore the Drift. Everyone else is exploring it for him.

**122** `[image: {kaiser} on {raft2_plain} at night reading many floating glowing chat reports at once, calm, organized like a CEO at his desk, {knight} standing guard]`
And on a raft in zone three thousand one hundred and eighteen, Kaiser is reading reports. Hundreds of them. One catches his eye. A zone where a man made a sea monster eat a raider.

**123** `[image: close-up of {kaiser}'s face lit blue by a chat window, a thin interested smile, cold hazel eyes]`
The zone number is two hundred and seventy-one thousand and ninety-four. The same zone as the name at the top of the World Ranking.

**124** `[image: {kaiser} turning slightly to speak to {knight} behind him at night, {knight} standing silent with his sword point-down, ominous]`
Kaiser doesn't post about it. He just says one thing to the silent knight behind him. "Find out what's on that raft. Everything."

**125** `[image: {knight} lowering his helmeted head in a slow bow at night, the blue visor slit glowing faintly]`
The knight bows his head. Kaiser has never needed to see something himself. He has twenty million people to see it for him.

**126** `[image: {aeliss} standing at the bow of {raft2} looking at the sky with a distant, worried expression, wind in her silver hair]`
That night, he asks Aeliss what her world's first event was. She's quiet for a long time. "Fire," she says finally. "Ours was fire. We didn't understand the hint until it was too late."

**127** `[image: {yunjae} and {aeliss} sitting side by side on night watch on {raft2}, the stars bright above, {hobb} asleep and snoring by the stove, peaceful]`
They take the night watch together. Hobb snores by the stove. The ocean is quiet. For once, nobody is hunting anybody.

**128** `[image: {aeliss} asleep leaning against {yunjae}'s shoulder on night watch, her silver hair spilling over his jacket, {yunjae} sitting very still and surprised, stars above]`
Some time after two, Aeliss falls asleep. And without meaning to, she ends up leaning against his shoulder.

**129** `[image: close-up of {yunjae} at night sitting stiffly, carefully not moving, a faint embarrassed flush, stars reflected in his eyes]`
Yun-jae doesn't move for three hours. He tells himself it's so she can rest.

At dawn she wakes up, sees where she is, and moves away without a word. But later, he notices the little pink bar in her status window has moved again.

## The riddle

**131** `[image: night sky over the ocean on day six, a huge glowing blue window appearing across the stars, abstract symbols, no readable text]`
Day six. At midnight, the hint arrives. Not in a private window. Across the entire sky.

**132** `[image: same]`
SYSTEM: I have no hands, yet I will knock on every roof. I have no feet, yet I will fill every path.

**133** `[image: same]`
SYSTEM: Tomorrow I will not stop to breathe. And all that lies low will drink until it drowns.

**134** `[image: dozens of glowing chat windows flickering frantically over the dark ocean, abstract, no readable text]`
Zone chat goes into a frenzy. Within minutes, there's a winning theory. "Fill every path." "Drink until it drowns." It's a tsunami. A giant wave is coming.

**135** `[image: comic montage of survivors on rafts at night arguing in glowing chat windows, one pointing at the sky, one holding up a bucket, one hiding under a plank, abstract symbols, no readable text]`
The other theories are less popular. One man is sure it's a giant hand that will knock on everyone's raft. Someone else thinks it's ghosts. One survivor just posts, "Whatever it is, I'm not ready," and everybody agrees.

**136** `[image: survivors on plain rafts at night tying themselves to their rafts with rope, frightened, a few stacking supplies in the middle, dim moonlight]`
People start tying themselves to their rafts. Stacking everything in the middle. Bracing for one huge wave.

**137** `[image: {yunjae} sitting on {raft2} at night with the sky glowing blue above him, mouthing the words of the riddle to himself, thoughtful]`
Yun-jae reads the riddle again. Slowly. Like a broken machine that's trying to tell him what's wrong with it.

**138** `[image: close-up of {yunjae}'s face at night, eyes sharp, piecing it together]`
Knock on every roof. A wave doesn't knock on roofs. Something that falls does. Fill every path, not all at once, but slowly. And it won't stop to breathe.

**139** `[image: rain drops falling on a calm ocean at night in close-up, each drop making a ring, moody]`
It's not a wave. It's rain. Rain that doesn't stop.

**140** `[image: close-up of {yunjae} at night tapping the raised edge of his raft, thinking, eyes narrowed]`
And the last line is the clue. "All that lies low will drink until it drowns." A raft is the lowest thing in the world. Fill it with water, and it doesn't matter how tightly you're tied to it.

**141** `[image: {yunjae} standing up on {raft2} at night, looking at his raft, the sail, the raised edges, calculating, determined]`
So the people tying themselves to their rafts are doing exactly the wrong thing. You don't need to hold on. You need to stay high. A roof. Drainage. Something to catch the water before it catches you.

**142** `[image: {yunjae} at night looking at the chat windows full of people tying themselves to rafts, hesitating, his fingers over a glowing window, conflicted]`
And then he has a choice to make. Ten thousand people in his zone are about to prepare for the wrong disaster. He could tell them.

**143** `[image: close-up of {yunjae} at night thinking, half his face lit blue, serious]`
But if Seo Yun-jae posts the answer, every raider in the zone learns two things: that he's clever, and that he's the one to follow, or to rob. Kindness, out here, has a price.

**144** `[image: {yunjae} typing a private message at night, a small determined smile, abstract symbols, no readable text]`
So he does both. He sends the answer privately to the one person who offered to say things out loud for him.

**145** `[image: {dahye} on a plain raft at night reading a message, then typing fast into zone chat, glowing windows multiplying around her, abstract symbols, no readable text]`
Ten minutes later, Yoon Da-hye posts in zone chat. "It's not a wave. It's rain that won't stop. Get your raft higher, build a roof, and catch the water before it catches you."

**146** `[image: survivors on plain rafts at night untying themselves and starting to stack planks into crude roofs, a few looking up at the sky, hopeful]`
Some people laugh at her. Some don't. Here and there across the dark, survivors start untying their ropes and building roofs instead.

**147** `[image: close-up of {yunjae} at night watching the chat with a quiet, satisfied look, the blue sky glow on his face]`
Yun-jae will never know exactly how many of them she saved. But he knows nobody will come looking for him because of it. Being careful and being kind, at the same time.

**148** `[image: close-up of the green carpenter card glowing in {yunjae}'s hand at night, his eyes reflecting the green light]`
He takes the Carpenter card out of his pocket. He knew he was saving it for something big.

On the World Ranking for day six, he's still number one. Kaiser is still number two. But the gap is smaller than it was. Twenty million people sending you their discoveries will do that.
