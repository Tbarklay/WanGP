# One Rank Higher, Episode 1: "The Raft"

Length: about 21-22 minutes (about 3,000 narrated words at a steady storytelling pace). Covers days 1 and 2.
No background music.
Each beat is one illustration, shown while its narration plays. `{yunjae}` and the other names in braces are filled in
with the matching description from `cast.json`, so every image draws the characters the same way.
The narration calls the hero "our MC" or Yun-jae, recap style. Lines starting SYSTEM: are read in the System's own voice;
`[image: same]` keeps the previous picture on screen for them. `[ui: ...]` after an image draws a status window or ranking board
as exact text over the picture (see stories/tools/ui_overlay.py).

---

## Cold open

**01** `[image: {yunjae} kneeling on {raft1} in the middle of an endless dark ocean at sunset, holding up a glowing card that is cracking open with brilliant purple light, the light reflecting on the water]`
Day one. Four billion people are lost at sea. And our MC is holding something that shouldn't exist yet.

**02** `[image: extreme close-up of a young man's smooth hand holding a glowing playing-card-sized card, its green frame cracking apart and purple light bursting through the cracks, dark ocean behind]`
A card that was green a second ago. Now it's turning purple.

**03** `[image: countless glowing blue holographic chat windows filling a night sky above the ocean, abstract scrolling symbols, no readable text]`
Somewhere, ten thousand people are about to start asking the same question. Who is this guy?

**04** `[image: pure black background with a single small glowing blue square of light in the center, minimal, no people]`
To understand how he got here, we have to go back to last night. Eleven fifty-nine p.m.

## The last night on Earth

**05** `[image: {yunjae} alone, the only person in the room, sitting at a kitchen table in a small cozy Seoul apartment at night repairing an old radio with a tiny screwdriver, tools spread out, warm lamp light]`
Our MC, Seo Yun-jae, is twenty-four years old, and he fixes things for a living. Phones, fridges, washing machines. If it's broken, he can make it work again.

**06** `[image: {grandma} sitting in an armchair in the apartment at night, smiling at an old radio on the table]`
Tonight it's his grandmother's radio. It's older than he is. She says it still sounds better than anything new.

**07** `[image: {haeun} fast asleep with her eyes closed on a sofa in the apartment at night, a school textbook open on her chest, a blanket half off]`
His little sister fell asleep studying again. She's fifteen. He's been raising her since their parents died, with their grandmother's help and a lot of overtime.

**08** `[image: close-up of the old radio crackling to life with a warm orange glow, {yunjae}'s hand on the dial]`
The radio crackles back to life. His grandmother claps. It's a small thing. Most of his life is small things, done well, for people who don't notice.

**09** `[image: a simple round wall clock with no numbers, only plain tick marks, its hands at one minute to midnight, warm lamp light in a cozy apartment, no text]`
Then the clock hits midnight.

**10** `[image: a giant glowing blue holographic panel appearing in the night sky above the Seoul skyline, everyone on the streets looking up, abstract symbols, no readable text]`
Every screen on Earth goes blue. Every phone, every TV, and the sky itself. One message, in every language at once.

**11** `[image: close-up of a floating translucent glowing blue holographic window hanging alone in empty dark space, only abstract glowing shapes, lines and icons on it, no people, no face, no person anywhere, no readable text, a dark room behind it]`
SYSTEM: The Trial begins. All candidates aged eighteen to sixty-five will now be transferred.

**12** `[image: {yunjae} standing up in the apartment, his body starting to glow with blue light and dissolve into particles, shock on his face]`
Yun-jae looks down. His hands are glowing. He's coming apart into light.

**13** `[image: {haeun} waking up on the sofa, reaching out toward an empty column of blue light in the middle of the room, terrified, she is alone in the room, no other people]`
His sister wakes up and screams his name. She reaches for him.

**14** `[image: {grandma} rising from her armchair, reaching out, blue light fading in front of her, tears in her eyes]`
His grandmother reaches for him too. Neither of them is glowing. Fifteen. Seventy-two. Too young and too old.

**15** `[image: a view from space of Earth at night with billions of tiny blue lights rising from its surface like fireflies]`
In one second, every adult between eighteen and sixty-five disappears from Earth. Four billion people. Everyone else is left behind.

## The Drift

**16** `[image: a busy Seoul street at night after the Summoning, empty cars with doors open, children and elderly people standing alone looking at the sky, shop signs blank and glowing with no lettering, no readable text]`
On Earth, the streets are suddenly full of empty cars, ringing phones, and kids standing alone on sidewalks, looking up at a sky that has already gone dark again.

**17** `[image: {yunjae} lying on his back on {raft} in the middle of an endless calm turquoise ocean under a bright sky, waking up, no land anywhere]`
Our MC wakes up on his back, staring at a sky that's too blue.

**18** `[image: wide high aerial shot of {yunjae} alone, small, standing on {raft} in a vast empty ocean stretching to the horizon in every direction, only one person]`
He's on a raft. Two meters by two meters. Four planks and some rope. And in every direction, as far as he can see, there is nothing but water.

**19** `[image: {raft} floating alone on the ocean seen from above, nobody aboard, its contents laid out on the bare planks: a simple wooden fishing rod, three spare wooden planks, a leather water flask]`
His inventory: one fishing rod. Three spare planks. One flask of water.

**20** `[image: {yunjae} sitting on {raft} in the ocean, squinting at a glowing blue holographic window floating in front of him with abstract shapes and no readable text, blue light on his face]`
Then a blue window pops up in front of his face. And it talks.

**21** `[image: close-up of a floating translucent glowing blue holographic window hanging alone in empty dark space, only abstract glowing shapes, lines and icons on it, no people, no face, no person anywhere, no readable text, glowing brightly over the ocean]`
SYSTEM: Good morning, candidate. You've been summoned to the Drift. Please try not to die. It makes the paperwork worse.

**22** `[image: {yunjae} sitting on {raft} in the ocean, staring at {panel} with a deadpan, unimpressed expression]`
Yun-jae stares at it. Of all the things that could talk to him at the end of the world, he got a sarcastic one.

## The rules

**23** `[image: a giant glowing map projection over the ocean showing thousands of tiny dots spread far apart, abstract, no readable text]`
The System explains the rules. Everyone was split into zones of ten thousand people, and every raft starts fifty kilometers from the next one. You can drift for days and never see another human.

**24** `[image: a giant red hourglass of light hanging in the sky over the ocean, ominous]`
When a zone drops to one thousand survivors, ten zones merge into one. Nobody needs the math explained. Nine out of ten people won't make it.

**25** `[image: close-up of a floating translucent glowing blue holographic window hanging alone in empty dark space, only abstract glowing shapes, lines and icons on it, no people, no face, no person anywhere, no readable text, with a glowing gift-box icon]`
Every day, the System hands out rewards. Every day, it ranks everyone. And every seven days, something called a World Event will come.

**26** `[image: extreme close-up of {yunjae}'s face filling the frame, head and shoulders only, lit by blue light from below, eyes narrowed in thought, dark background]`
It doesn't say what a World Event is. He notices that. He notices it doesn't say what happens to the people who don't survive one, either. He has always been the kind of person who notices what's missing.

## Zone chat

**27** `[image: {yunjae} on {raft} with dozens of small glowing blue chat windows floating around him like fireflies, abstract scrolling symbols, no readable text]`
Then the chats open. Zone chat, for the ten thousand people in his zone. And world chat, for all four billion, where the System lets each person post only once an hour. Both are pure panic.

**28** `[image: {taegu} standing on {raft} in the ocean flexing both arms, his skin glowing like polished metal, proud grin]`
Abilities, it turns out, match your personality. A gym guy got Iron Body. He's posting pictures of himself flexing on a raft.

**29** `[image: {dahye} kneeling on {raft} in the ocean, her hands glowing with soft green healing light, determined expression]`
A nurse got Mend. A guy who never threw anything away got Infinite Pocket.

**30** `[image: {jiho_vanish} on {raft}, his whole body semi-transparent like a ghost, the ocean clearly visible through his body, looking around fearfully]`
And somebody who admits he's a coward got Vanish. Chat roasts him for an hour.

**31** `[image: {yunjae} opening a glowing blue panel on his own wrist, curious expression, the ocean behind him]`
Then he checks his own.

**32** `[image: close-up of a glowing blue panel with a single bright glowing hammer-and-star icon at its center, abstract, no readable text]`
SYSTEM: Refine. Raise the rank of any item by one. Rarely, by two. Each item can only be refined once. Three uses per day.

**33** `[image: {yunjae} sitting on {raft} in the ocean, looking at a floating blue holographic window of abstract glowing lines and shapes with a disappointed, deflated expression, the endless ocean behind him]`
He reads it twice, hoping he missed something. He didn't. He was hoping for something like Iron Body. Something that would make him stronger, faster, harder to kill. What he got doesn't make him anything. It only makes his things better.

**34** `[image: {yunjae} looking down at {raft} under him and the plain fishing rod in his hand, the empty ocean in every direction]`
And right now, his things are four planks, a fishing rod and one flask of water.

**35** `[image: {yunjae} sitting hunched on {raft}, arms around his knees, staring at the endless ocean with a worried, nervous expression]`
That's when it really sinks in. Somewhere out there, people are turning their skin into iron. He can make a plank a little nicer. Alone, in the middle of an ocean that never ends, he has no idea how he's supposed to survive with that.

**36** `[image: {yunjae} frowning up at the floating {panel}, frustrated, one hand raised like asking a question]`
So he asks the System straight out. Why did he get stuck with a support ability?

**37** `[image: close-up of a floating translucent glowing blue holographic window hanging alone in empty dark space, only abstract glowing shapes, lines and icons on it, no people, no face, no person anywhere, no readable text, glowing brightly over the ocean]`
SYSTEM: Abilities aren't handed out at random. Each one is shaped by the person who receives it. Your personality chose this one.

**38** `[image: same]`
SYSTEM: Apparently, you can't leave anything alone until it's better.

**39** `[image: {yunjae} sitting on {raft} in the ocean in daylight, giving a small reluctant smile, looking at his hands]`
Yun-jae wants to argue. He can't. Every broken radio, every cracked phone, every dead fridge he ever brought back to life says the System is right. He just wishes his personality had come with a sword.

## Fishing

**40** `[image: an open white treasure chest sitting on an empty tiny wooden raft floating alone on the ocean, nobody aboard, the chest holding a loaf of bread, a water flask and a small pouch of glowing fishing bait]`
His first daily login reward drops onto the raft: a white chest with bread, water and a pouch of bait. Not exciting. But the System hints that the ocean has better things in it, if you can pull them out.

**41** `[image: {yunjae} sitting on the edge of {raft} casting a thin simple wooden fishing rod with a line into calm turquoise water, a bright sky]`
So he fishes. The first fish takes an hour. The second takes another.

**42** `[image: {yunjae} on {raft} holding the plain fishing rod up and studying it critically, two small fish beside him]`
Two fish in two hours. Yun-jae looks at the rod the way he'd look at a broken machine on his workbench. It's the bottleneck. A better rod means better catches, and better catches mean everything else.

**43** `[image: a simple wooden fishing rod glowing with green light and transforming into a sleek polished rod with silver rings]`
"Refine." The rod glows green. Uncommon. Lighter, longer, and the line hums like a guitar string. His first Refine of the day, spent on the one thing that will pay him back every single hour.

**44** `[image: {yunjae} sitting on {raft} pulling hard on a bent silver-ringed fishing rod, the line taut, a glowing white light under the water at the end of the line]`
The very next cast, the line goes heavy, and something white is glowing under the water.

**45** `[image: an open white treasure chest sitting on an empty tiny wooden raft floating alone on the ocean, nobody aboard, the chest holding two wooden planks, a coil of rope, a length of polished bamboo and a spool of waxed fishing line]`
A white chest. Inside: two planks, a coil of rope, and a little bundle the System calls rod parts. A length of polished bamboo, and a spool of waxed line.

**46** `[image: close-up of a floating translucent glowing blue holographic window hanging alone in empty dark space, only abstract glowing shapes, lines and icons on it, no people, no face, no person anywhere, no readable text, next to a floating bamboo rod section and a spool of fishing line over the ocean]`
With these, the System says, anyone can upgrade a basic rod to Uncommon the old-fashioned way. No ability needed.

**47** `[image: {yunjae} alone on {raft} holding a bamboo rod section and a spool of line, a slow thoughtful smile, the ocean glittering behind him, only one person]`
His rod is already Uncommon. He doesn't need them. And that's exactly what makes them valuable. Right now, every person on Earth is fishing with the same bad rod.

## The trade

**48** `[image: {yunjae} on {raft} typing into a glowing blue chat window, calm and focused, abstract symbols, no readable text]`
So he does something nobody else has thought of yet. He spends his one world chat post for the hour. "Have rod upgrade parts. Bamboo and waxed line. Looking for something I don't have."

**49** `[image: hundreds of glowing blue chat windows exploding into the sky like fireworks over the ocean, abstract symbols, no readable text]`
World chat explodes. Hundreds of offers in seconds. Fish. Bread. Water. A pair of sunglasses. One man offers his entire raft, which would be a very strange way to die.

**50** `[image: {yunjae} sitting on {raft} in the ocean, calmly scrolling past a flood of small glowing blue windows with a flat expression, no readable text]`
He turns every one of them down. He already has fish, bread and water. He isn't looking for more of what everyone has. He's looking for the thing nobody knows the value of yet.

**51** `[image: {jiho} sitting alone on {raft}, typing nervously into a small glowing chat window, abstract symbols, no readable text]`
Then a private message arrives. It's from the guy with Vanish, the one zone chat spent an hour roasting. His name is Noh Ji-ho. "Would you take a common citizen card?"

**52** `[image: close-up of {yunjae}'s face, eyebrows raised in sharp curiosity, blue chat light on his face]`
A citizen card. Yun-jae has never heard of one. That alone makes it worth more than every fish in world chat. He asks where Ji-ho got it. "From a white chest," Ji-ho writes. "Fishing. Same as you."

**53** `[image: {jiho} sitting alone on {raft} holding a glowing white card, looking down at a single small fish beside him, worried]`
So why trade it away? Ji-ho's answer is honest. "It's a cook. I can barely catch enough to feed myself. I can't feed a second mouth."

**54** `[image: a glowing blue trade window floating over the ocean, a bamboo rod section and a spool of line dissolving into light on one side, a white glowing card appearing on the other, abstract, no readable text]`
The System opens a trade window between them. The bamboo and the line dissolve from his hands into light, and a white card appears in their place. Distance doesn't matter. The System delivers.

**55** `[image: six glowing cards in a neat row from left to right: a white card, a green card, a blue card, a purple card, a gold card and a red card, each framed in its own colored light, dramatic dark background, no text]`
Then the System explains. A citizen card summons a person to live and work for you. Citizens come in six ranks: white, common. Green, uncommon. Blue, rare. Purple, epic. Gold, legendary. And red. Apex.

**56** `[image: close-up of a floating translucent glowing blue holographic window hanging alone in empty dark space, only abstract glowing shapes, lines and icons on it, no people, no face, no person anywhere, no readable text, its glow dimming slightly]`
He asks where citizens come from. The System pauses for a second.

**57** `[image: same]`
SYSTEM: From fallen worlds.

**58** `[image: same]`
He doesn't ask what that means. He has a feeling he'll find out.

**59** `[image: close-up of a young man's smooth hand holding a glowing card with a white frame, a silhouette of a plump cook with a ladle on it, soft white glow, dark ocean behind, no readable text]`
The card says Cook. Common. He doesn't activate it. Ji-ho was right: an extra mouth needs food he doesn't have yet. It goes into his inventory. Later.

**60** `[image: {yunjae} sitting on {raft} in the ocean, typing into a small glowing blue holographic window floating in front of him, a faint smile, no laptop, no readable text]`
Before the window closes, he adds Ji-ho as a friend and sends one more message. "If you ever find something valuable or unusual that you don't want, message me first."

**61** `[image: {jiho} on {raft} far away, reading a glowing message in his hands with a small surprised smile]`
On a raft somewhere far away, the guy everyone laughed at reads that twice. Nobody has asked him for anything all day.

**62** `[image: {yunjae} sitting on {raft} with the white card and his new rod, looking out at the horizon like a man planning several moves ahead]`
Half a day in, Yun-jae has turned two things he didn't need into a citizen card, a contact, and the first thread of a network. Everyone else is still trying to catch dinner.

## The green chest

**63** `[image: {yunjae} sitting on {raft} in the ocean in daylight, holding up a single plain wooden plank, examining it closely, thoughtful]`
Two Refines left. He spends the second on the most important thing he owns: the raft under him. One plank.

**64** `[image: a plain wooden plank glowing with soft green light, its wood grain turning smooth and strong, sparkles around it]`
"Refine." The wood tightens, darkens, smooths out. White to green. Common plank to Sturdy Plank.

**65** `[image: {yunjae} pressing his hand on a glowing green plank fitted into {raft1}, impressed expression]`
He fits it into the raft and presses down. It's like stepping on concrete. One plank, and it's the strongest part of his raft.

**66** `[image: {yunjae} sitting on {raft1} at late afternoon with a short plain polished wooden fishing rod with no reel laid flat across his knees, both hands resting on it, small silver line rings on the rod, gazing at the horizon thoughtfully]`
And he realizes something the rest of the world hasn't yet. Everyone's ability is fixed. Iron Body will always be Iron Body. His makes everything else better. The longer this goes on, the bigger that gap gets.

**67** `[image: a glowing green treasure chest rising out of the water on the end of a fishing line, seawater streaming off it]`
He casts again. Twenty minutes later, a green chest. His first uncommon.

**68** `[image: close-up of a young man's smooth hand holding a glowing card with a green frame, on the card a small silhouette of a robed healer holding a small lantern, soft green glow, dark ocean behind, no other people, no readable text]`
Inside: another card. Green this time. A Wandering Healer, uncommon. Most people in the zone will be lucky to find a white.

## Refine

**69** `[image: {yunjae} holding the green card in one hand, his other hand hovering over it with faint blue light gathering, determined expression]`
One Refine left today. And now he has two cards.

**70** `[image: close-up of {yunjae}'s eyes reflecting green light, determined]`
The white Cook could become a green one. Useful. But the green Healer could become blue: a rare citizen on day one. He picks the bigger gamble and takes a breath.

**71** `[image: the green card glowing intensely and turning blue, blue light flooding out of it]`
"Refine." The green frame flickers, and turns blue. Rare. Exactly what he wanted.

**72** `[image: the blue glowing card suddenly cracking down the middle, intense purple light bursting through the cracks, the ocean lit purple around it]`
Then it doesn't stop. The blue frame cracks. And purple light pours out of it.

**73** `[image: close-up of a floating translucent glowing blue holographic window hanging alone in empty dark space, only abstract glowing shapes, lines and icons on it, no people, no face, no person anywhere, no readable text, glowing purple at the edges]`
SYSTEM: Luck triggered. Plus two ranks. Uncommon to Epic.

**74** `[image: a brilliant purple card glowing in {yunjae}'s hands on {raft1}, the card shows a small silhouette of an elegant figure with long hair and a staff, purple light on his amazed face, only one person]`
The Wandering Healer is gone. The card has a new name now. Aeliss. Tide-Caller of the Silver Shore. Epic.

## Aeliss

**75** `[image: {yunjae} alone on {raft1} at sunset shielding his eyes as a pillar of purple and aqua light rises from the middle of the raft into the sky, only one person]`
He activates it. A column of purple light rises off the raft, so bright that rafts fifty kilometers away see it on the horizon.

**76** `[image: {aeliss} standing alone on a tiny wooden raft of four planks, one plank glowing soft green, purple light fading around her, silver hair floating, eyes closed, the sunset ocean behind her, full body]`
When it fades, a woman is standing on his raft. Silver hair. Pointed ears. A staff with a pearl that glows like the sea.

**77** `[image: {aeliss} opening her sea-green eyes and looking at the endless ocean, a sad knowing expression]`
She opens her eyes. She looks at the ocean. And she isn't surprised. Not even a little.

**78** `[image: {aeliss} kneeling on one knee on {raft1} before {yunjae}, head slightly bowed, calm and composed, sunset behind her]`
Then she turns to our MC, kneels on one knee, and says two words. "Hello, master."

**79** `[image: {yunjae} standing awkwardly on {raft1}, scratching the back of his head, unsure what to do, {aeliss} kneeling silently before him]`
That's it. Nothing else. Yun-jae has never been called master in his life. He waits for her to say more. She doesn't. She just watches him, calm and patient, like she's waiting for orders.

## Status

**80** `[image: {yunjae} raising his hand toward {aeliss}, a glowing blue status window opening in the air beside her, abstract glowing bars, no readable text]`
So he tries something. He focuses on her, the way he focuses on the System, and a window opens in the air beside her. Her status. Every citizen has one.

**81** `[image: close-up of a glowing blue holographic status window with four horizontal bars glowing red, orange, green and a long bright blue bar, dark empty background, no people, no face, no readable text]` `[ui: status | Aeliss | Tide-Caller of the Silver Shore · EPIC | HP 70/100 | STR 14/100 | AGI 31/100 | MANA 97/100 | Loyalty 60% | Affinity 5%]`
Health. Strength. Agility. Mana. Her strength and agility are nothing special. But her mana bar runs almost off the edge of the window. She isn't a fighter. She's something rarer.

**82** `[image: close-up of a glowing blue holographic window with a single glowing gold bar about two thirds full, dark empty background, no people, no face, no readable text]` `[ui: status | Aeliss | Bound citizen · EPIC | Loyalty 60%]`
Below that, two more numbers. The first is Loyalty: sixty percent. Summoned citizens arrive bound to their master, but not devoted. That part has to be earned.

**83** `[image: close-up of a glowing blue holographic window with a single thin soft pink glowing bar almost empty, dark empty background, no people, no face, no readable text]` `[ui: status | Aeliss | Bound citizen · EPIC | Affinity 5%]`
And the last one is a soft pink bar he's never heard of. Affinity: five percent.

**84** `[image: close-up of a floating translucent glowing blue holographic window hanging alone in empty dark space, only abstract glowing shapes, lines and icons on it, no people, no face, no person anywhere, no readable text, glowing beside a smaller window with colored bars]`
The System explains, in its usual dry way.

**85** `[image: same]`
SYSTEM: Affinity is how a citizen feels about you, personally. It only shows for citizens of the opposite sex. And what it unlocks at higher levels is between the two of you.

**86** `[image: {yunjae} coughing and looking away with a red face, {aeliss} beside him calm and expressionless, sunset light]`
Our MC coughs and closes the window. That's a problem for much, much later. Aeliss doesn't react at all.

**87** `[image: {yunjae} sitting across from {aeliss} on {raft1} at sunset, asking her a question, curious and careful]`
So he starts asking questions. And he learns something about her quickly. She answers anything he asks, honestly, right away. She just never says anything he didn't ask for.

**88** `[image: close-up portrait of {aeliss}'s face alone, a single tear on her cheek, calm, sunset light, only one person]`
Where is she from? "From a world that fell, master," she says quietly. "It fell on its twenty-eighth day."

**89** `[image: {yunjae} staring at {aeliss}, stunned, the meaning sinking in]`
Fallen worlds. Citizens. Yun-jae is sharing a raft with the survivor of a world that lost this exact game.

**90** `[image: {aeliss} kneeling at the edge of {raft1} and touching the water with her staff, aqua light rippling outward across the ocean]`
He asks what she can do. She doesn't explain. She just kneels and touches the water with her staff, and light ripples out across the waves.

**91** `[image: a huge silver school of fish swirling up around {raft1} in the clear turquoise water seen from above, nobody on the raft, no people, a faint glow far below in the deep water]`
And the sea answers. Fish rise around the raft by the hundreds. Somewhere far below them, something faintly glows. She notices it. She doesn't mention it. Not yet.

**92** `[image: {yunjae} and {aeliss} sitting on {raft1} at dusk eating grilled fish, quiet and calm, the sea golden around them]`
They eat grilled fish as the sun goes down, mostly in silence. When he asks what she was, back home, she answers: "The one who called the tides for my city." Then nothing more.

**93** `[image: close-up of a slender hand on a glowing coral staff, the pearl pulsing aqua, only the hand and the staff, no face]`
Later, he asks the question that's been bothering him. What happens to her if he dies? She tells him the truth, in the same quiet voice. Citizens go free. Free, and alone, on an ocean with no one.

**94** `[image: night falls over the ocean, a giant glowing holographic leaderboard appearing across the entire starry sky, abstract glowing rows, no readable text]`
At midnight, the first daily ranking appears. Not on a screen. Across the whole sky, for every survivor to see.

**95** `[image: close-up of a giant glowing holographic board in the night sky, its top row blazing gold, only glowing bars of light, no people, no face, no numbers, no letters, no readable text]` `[ui: ranking | World Ranking · Day 1 · Top 10 | 1 Seo Yun-jae (Zone 271,094) | 2 Kaiser (Zone 3,118) | 3 Lena Hartmann (Zone 88,402) | 4 Mateus Oliveira (Zone 207,465) | 5 Putri Wulandari (Zone 152,770) | 6 Arjun Mehta (Zone 61,837) | 7 Amira Haddad (Zone 118,903) | 8 Kwame Mensah (Zone 340,216) | 9 Dilnoza Karimova (Zone 9,551) | 10 Sofia Reyes (Zone 395,028)]`
Number one in the world: Seo Yun-jae. Achievement: first Epic citizen summoned in any zone.

**96** `[image: {yunjae} sitting on {raft1} at night looking up at a blazing gold streak of light in the starry sky, {aeliss} beside him, no letters, no readable text]`
Our MC looks up at his own name in the sky. Four billion people are looking at it too.

**97** `[image: dozens of small glowing chat windows exploding into the night sky like fireworks, abstract symbols, no readable text]`
Zone chat explodes. Who is Seo Yun-jae? What's his ability? Where is he? That last question makes him a little uneasy.

## Number two

**98** `[image: {kaiser} standing on a plain wooden raft on a dark ocean at night, looking up at the sky, {knight} kneeling on one knee beside him with his sword point-down]`
Somewhere in a different zone, a man is standing on his raft, looking up at the same sky.

**99** `[image: close-up of a giant glowing holographic board in the night sky, its second row glowing silver, only glowing bars of light, no people, no face, no numbers, no letters, no readable text]`
Number two in the world: Kaiser.

**100** `[image: head-and-shoulders bust portrait of {kaiser}, one single man, his face filling the frame, lit by a golden glow from above, cold amber eyes, a thin smile forming, dark night sky behind]`
He used to run one of the biggest companies in the world. He has never come second at anything. And for the first time since the Summoning, he smiles.

**101** `[image: extreme close-up of {kaiser}'s open hand, palm up, a dark red glow rising from it with thin glowing red chains of light wrapping his fingers, dark background, ominous, no other people, no machines]`
