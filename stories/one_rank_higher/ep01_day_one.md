# One Rank Higher, Episode 1: "The Raft"

Length: about 21-22 minutes (about 3,000 narrated words at a steady storytelling pace). Covers days 1 and 2.
No background music.
Each beat is one illustration, shown while its narration plays. `{yunjae}` and the other names in braces are filled in
with the matching description from `cast.json`, so every image draws the characters the same way.
The narration calls the hero "our MC", recap style; his name appears in System messages and rankings.

---

Day one. Four billion people are lost at sea. And our MC is holding something that shouldn't exist yet.

**02** `[image: extreme close-up of a glowing playing-card-sized card in a hand, its green frame cracking apart and purple light bursting through the cracks]`

**04** `[image: pure black background with a single small glowing blue square of light in the center, minimal, no people]`

## The last night on Earth

**05** `[image: a small cozy apartment in Seoul at night, warm lamp light, {yunjae} sitting at a kitchen table repairing an old radio with a tiny screwdriver, tools spread out]`
Our MC is twenty-four years old, and he fixes things for a living. Phones, fridges, washing machines. If it's broken, he can make it work again.

**06** `[image: {grandma} sitting in an armchair in the apartment at night, smiling at an old radio on the table]`
Tonight it's his grandmother's radio. It's older than he is. She says it still sounds better than anything new.

**07** `[image: {haeun} asleep on a sofa in the apartment at night, a school textbook open on her chest, a blanket half off]`
His little sister fell asleep studying again. She's fifteen. He's been raising her since their parents died, with their grandmother's help and a lot of overtime.

**08** `[image: close-up of the old radio crackling to life with a warm orange glow, {yunjae}'s hand on the dial]`
The radio crackles back to life. His grandmother claps. It's a small thing. Most of his life is small things, done well, for people who don't notice.

**09** `[image: a wall clock in the apartment showing one minute to midnight, warm lamp light]`
Then the clock hits midnight.

**10** `[image: a giant glowing blue holographic panel appearing in the night sky above the Seoul skyline, everyone on the streets looking up, abstract symbols, no readable text]`
Every screen on Earth goes blue. Every phone, every TV, and the sky itself. One message, in every language at once.

**11** `[image: close-up of a floating glowing blue holographic panel in a dark room, abstract symbols, no readable text]`
"The Trial begins. All candidates aged eighteen to sixty-five will now be transferred."

**12** `[image: {yunjae} standing up in the apartment, his body starting to glow with blue light and dissolve into particles, shock on his face]`
Our MC looks down. His hands are glowing. He's coming apart into light.

**13** `[image: {haeun} waking up on the sofa, reaching out toward a column of blue light where her brother was standing, terrified]`
His sister wakes up and screams his name. She reaches for him.

**14** `[image: {grandma} rising from her armchair, reaching out, blue light fading in front of her, tears in her eyes]`
His grandmother reaches for him too. Neither of them is glowing. Fifteen. Seventy-two. Too young and too old.

**15** `[image: a view from space of Earth at night with billions of tiny blue lights rising from its surface like fireflies]`
In one second, every adult between eighteen and sixty-five disappears from Earth. Four billion people. Everyone else is left behind.

## The Drift

**16** `[image: a busy Seoul street at night after the Summoning, empty cars with doors open, children and elderly people standing alone looking at the sky]`
On Earth, the streets are suddenly full of empty cars, ringing phones, and kids standing alone on sidewalks, looking up at a sky that has already gone dark again.

**17** `[image: {yunjae} lying on his back on {raft} in the middle of an endless calm turquoise ocean under a bright sky, waking up, no land anywhere]`
Our MC wakes up on his back, staring at a sky that's too blue.

**18** `[image: wide high aerial shot of {raft} alone in a vast empty ocean stretching to the horizon in every direction]`
He's on a raft. Two meters by two meters. Four planks and some rope. And in every direction, as far as he can see, there is nothing but water.

**19** `[image: {raft} seen from above with its contents laid out on it: a simple wooden fishing rod, three spare wooden planks, a leather water flask]`
His inventory: one fishing rod. Three spare planks. One flask of water.

**20** `[image: {panel} appearing in front of {yunjae} on {raft}, blue light on his face, he squints at it]`
Then a blue window pops up in front of his face. And it talks.

**21** `[image: close-up of {panel} glowing brightly, abstract symbols, no readable text]`
"Good morning, candidate. You've been summoned to the Drift. Please try not to die. It makes the paperwork worse."

**22** `[image: {yunjae} staring at {panel} with a deadpan, unimpressed expression]`
Our MC stares at it. Of all the things that could talk to him at the end of the world, he got a sarcastic one.

## The rules

**23** `[image: a giant glowing map projection over the ocean showing thousands of tiny dots spread far apart, abstract, no readable text]`
The System explains the rules. Everyone was split into zones of ten thousand people. And every raft in a zone starts fifty kilometers from the next one.

**24** `[image: two tiny rafts like {raft} far apart on a vast ocean at dusk, barely visible to each other on the horizon]`
You can drift for days and never see another human. Or you can run into one by accident. The System says that like it's a good thing.

**25** `[image: a frightened man on {raft} paddling with his hands toward a distant speck on the horizon, tired and desperate]`
In the first hour, a lot of people try paddling with their hands toward anything on the horizon. Most of it turns out to be clouds.

**26** `[image: a giant red hourglass of light hanging in the sky over the ocean, ominous]`
When a zone drops to one thousand survivors, ten zones merge into one. Nobody needs the math explained. Nine out of ten people won't make it.

**27** `[image: close-up of {panel} with a glowing gift-box icon and a list of abstract symbols, no readable text]`
Every day, the System hands out rewards. Every day, it ranks everyone. And every seven days, something called a World Event will come.

**28** `[image: close-up of {yunjae}'s face lit by the blue panel, eyes narrowed in thought]`
It doesn't say what a World Event is. Our MC notices that. He notices it doesn't say what happens to the people who don't survive one, either.

## Zone chat

**29** `[image: {yunjae} on {raft} with dozens of small glowing blue chat windows floating around him like fireflies, abstract scrolling symbols, no readable text]`
Then zone chat opens. Ten thousand panicking people, all typing at once.

**30** `[image: {taegu} standing on {raft} in the ocean flexing both arms, his skin glowing like polished metal, proud grin]`
Abilities, it turns out, match your personality. A gym guy got Iron Body. He's posting pictures of himself flexing on a raft.

**31** `[image: {dahye} kneeling on {raft} in the ocean, her hands glowing with soft green healing light, determined expression]`
A nurse got Mend. A guy who never threw anything away got Infinite Pocket.

**32** `[image: a nervous young man on {raft} turning half transparent, looking around fearfully]`
And somebody who admits he's a coward got Vanish. Chat roasts him for an hour.

**33** `[image: a smiling woman on {raft} holding a frying pan with glowing orange light around it, a fish sizzling on it over nothing]`
A woman who loved cooking got Hearth. She can cook a fish on a frying pan with no fire. For the first time all day, zone chat is jealous of something useful.

**34** `[image: {yunjae} opening a glowing blue panel on his own wrist, curious expression, the ocean behind him]`
Our MC checks his own ability.

**35** `[image: close-up of a glowing blue panel with a single bright glowing hammer-and-star icon at its center, abstract, no readable text]`
"Refine. Raise the rank of any item by one. Rarely, by two. Each item can only be refined once. Three uses per day."

**36** `[image: {yunjae} looking at the panel with a disappointed, deflated expression, the endless ocean behind him]`
He reads it twice, hoping he missed something. He didn't. He was hoping for something like Iron Body. Something that would make him stronger, faster, harder to kill. What he got doesn't make him anything. It only makes his things better.

**37** `[image: {yunjae} looking down at {raft} under him and the plain fishing rod in his hand, the empty ocean in every direction]`
And right now, his things are four planks, a fishing rod and one flask of water.

**38** `[image: {yunjae} sitting hunched on {raft}, arms around his knees, staring at the endless ocean with a worried, nervous expression]`
That's when it really sinks in. Somewhere out there, people are turning their skin into iron. Our MC can make a plank a little nicer. Alone, in the middle of an ocean that never ends, he has no idea how he's supposed to survive with that.

**39** `[image: {yunjae} frowning up at the floating {panel}, frustrated, one hand raised like asking a question]`
So he asks the System straight out. Why did he get stuck with a support ability?

**40** `[image: close-up of {panel} glowing brightly, abstract symbols, no readable text]`
"Abilities aren't handed out at random," the System replies. "Each one is shaped by the person who receives it. Your personality chose this one." A pause. "Apparently, you can't leave anything alone until it's better."

**41** `[image: {yunjae} giving a small reluctant smile, looking at his hands]`
Our MC wants to argue. He can't. Every broken radio, every cracked phone, every dead fridge he ever brought back to life says the System is right. He just wishes his personality had come with a sword.

## Login reward

**42** `[image: a small glowing white treasure chest appearing on {raft} with a soft burst of white light]`
His first daily login reward drops onto the raft. A white chest. Common.

**43** `[image: an open white chest on {raft} holding a loaf of bread, a water flask and a small pouch of glowing fishing bait]`
Bread, water, and a pouch of bait. Not exciting. But the System hints that the ocean has better things in it. You just have to pull them out.

## Fishing

**44** `[image: {yunjae} sitting on the edge of {raft} casting a simple wooden fishing rod into calm turquoise water, a bright sky]`
So our MC fishes. The first hour, he catches a fish. The second hour, he catches another fish.

**45** `[image: {yunjae} pulling hard on a bent fishing rod, a glowing white light under the water at the end of the line]`
Third hour, the line goes heavy, and something white is glowing under the water.

**46** `[image: a small white treasure chest dripping with seawater being pulled onto {raft}]`
A chest. White again. Inside: two planks and a coil of rope.

**47** `[image: {yunjae} holding up a single plain wooden plank, examining it closely, thoughtful]`
And that's when our MC decides to test his ability. Not on anything important. On a plank.

**48** `[image: a plain wooden plank glowing with soft green light, its wood grain turning smooth and strong, sparkles around it]`
"Refine." The plank glows. The wood tightens, darkens, smooths out. White to green. Common plank to Sturdy Plank.

**49** `[image: {yunjae} pressing his hand on a glowing green plank fitted into {raft1}, impressed expression]`
He fits it into the raft and presses down. It's like stepping on concrete. One plank, and it's the strongest part of his raft.

**50** `[image: {yunjae} sitting on {raft1} holding the fishing rod and looking at it thoughtfully, the sea glittering]`
Two uses left today. He looks at his fishing rod. A better rod, better catches. Probably.

**51** `[image: a simple wooden fishing rod glowing with green light and transforming into a sleek polished rod with silver rings]`
Refine. The rod glows green. Uncommon. Lighter, longer, and the line hums like a guitar string.

## The green chest

**52** `[image: {yunjae} on {raft1} watching the horizon thoughtfully, the silver-ringed rod in his hands, late afternoon light]`
Our MC realizes something the rest of zone chat hasn't yet. Everyone's ability is fixed. Iron Body will always be Iron Body. But his makes everything else better. The longer this goes on, the bigger that gap gets.

**53** `[image: {yunjae} casting the new silver-ringed fishing rod into the ocean at golden late afternoon]`
He casts again. This time, it takes twenty minutes.

**54** `[image: a glowing green treasure chest rising out of the water on the end of a fishing line, seawater streaming off it]`
A green chest. His first uncommon. Inside, something flat and glowing.

**55** `[image: close-up of a hand holding a glowing card with a green frame, a silhouette of a robed figure on it, soft green glow, no readable text]`
A card. A green frame, and the shape of a person.

**56** `[image: close-up of {panel} glowing beside a green card, abstract symbols, no readable text]`
The System explains. "Citizen card. Activate it to summon a citizen, a person who will live and work for you."

**57** `[image: six glowing cards fanned out in a row, framed in white, green, blue, purple, gold and red light, dramatic dark background]`
Citizens come in six ranks. White, common. Green, uncommon. Blue, rare. Purple, epic. Gold, legendary. And red. Apex.

**58** `[image: close-up of the green card, the silhouette of a robed healer holding a small lantern, soft green glow]`
This one is green. A Wandering Healer. A decent card for day one. Most people in the zone will be lucky to find a white.

**59** `[image: {yunjae} asking the floating {panel} a question, curious expression]`
Our MC asks one question. "Where do citizens come from?"

**60** `[image: close-up of {panel}, its glow dimming slightly, abstract symbols, no readable text]`
The System pauses for a second. "From fallen worlds."

**61** `[image: {yunjae} looking down at the card in his hand, serious expression, the sun low behind him]`
He doesn't ask what that means. He has a feeling he'll find out.

## Refine

**62** `[image: {yunjae} holding the green card in one hand, his other hand hovering over it with faint blue light gathering, determined expression]`
He has one use of Refine left today. And he has a card.

**63** `[image: close-up of {yunjae}'s eyes reflecting green light, determined]`
Green to blue would be good. A rare citizen on day one. He takes a breath.

**64** `[image: the green card glowing intensely and turning blue, blue light flooding out of it]`
"Refine." The green frame flickers, and turns blue. Rare. Exactly what he wanted.

**65** `[image: the blue glowing card suddenly cracking down the middle, intense purple light bursting through the cracks, the ocean lit purple around it]`
Then it doesn't stop. The blue frame cracks. And purple light pours out of it.

**66** `[image: close-up of {panel} glowing purple at the edges, abstract symbols, no readable text]`
"Luck triggered. Plus two ranks. Uncommon to Epic."

**67** `[image: a brilliant purple card glowing in {yunjae}'s hands, the silhouette on it now an elegant figure with long hair and a staff, purple light on his amazed face]`
The Wandering Healer is gone. The card has a new name now. Aeliss. Tide-Caller of the Silver Shore. Epic.

## Aeliss

**68** `[image: a pillar of purple and aqua light rising from {raft1} into the sky over the ocean at sunset]`
He activates it. A column of purple light rises off the raft, so bright that rafts fifty kilometers away see it on the horizon.

**69** `[image: {aeliss} standing on {raft1} as purple light fades around her, silver hair floating, eyes closed, the sunset ocean behind her]`
When it fades, a woman is standing on his raft. Silver hair. Pointed ears. A staff with a pearl that glows like the sea.

**70** `[image: {aeliss} opening her sea-green eyes and looking at the endless ocean, a sad knowing expression]`
She opens her eyes. She looks at the ocean. And she isn't surprised. Not even a little.

**71** `[image: {aeliss} kneeling on one knee on {raft1} before {yunjae}, head slightly bowed, calm and composed, sunset behind her]`
Then she turns to our MC, kneels on one knee, and says two words. "Hello, master."

**72** `[image: {yunjae} standing awkwardly on {raft1}, scratching the back of his head, unsure what to do, {aeliss} kneeling silently before him]`
That's it. Nothing else. Our MC has never been called master in his life. He waits for her to say more. She doesn't. She just watches him, calm and patient, like she's waiting for orders.

## Status

**73** `[image: {yunjae} raising his hand toward {aeliss}, a glowing blue status window opening in the air beside her, abstract glowing bars, no readable text]`
So he tries something. He focuses on her, the way he focuses on the System, and a window opens in the air beside her. Her status. Every citizen has one.

**74** `[image: close-up of a glowing blue holographic status window with four horizontal bars glowing red, orange, green and a long bright blue bar, abstract, no readable text]`
Health. Strength. Agility. Mana. Her strength and agility are nothing special. But her mana bar runs almost off the edge of the window. She isn't a fighter. She's something rarer.

**75** `[image: close-up of the status window's lower section, a glowing gold bar about two thirds full, abstract, no readable text]`
Below that, two more numbers. The first is Loyalty: sixty percent. Summoned citizens arrive bound to their master, but not devoted. That part has to be earned.

**76** `[image: close-up of the status window's last row, a thin soft pink glowing bar almost empty, abstract, no readable text]`
And the last one is a soft pink bar he's never heard of. Affinity: five percent.

**77** `[image: close-up of {panel} glowing beside the status window, abstract symbols, no readable text]`
The System explains, in its usual dry way. Affinity is how a citizen feels about you, personally. It only shows for citizens of the opposite sex. And what it unlocks at higher levels, the System adds, "is between the two of you."

**78** `[image: {yunjae} coughing and looking away with a red face, {aeliss} beside him calm and expressionless, sunset light]`
Our MC coughs and closes the window. That's a problem for much, much later. Aeliss doesn't react at all.

**79** `[image: {yunjae} sitting across from {aeliss} on {raft1} at sunset, asking her a question, curious and careful]`
So he starts asking questions. And he learns something about her quickly. She answers anything he asks, honestly, right away. She just never says anything he didn't ask for.

**80** `[image: close-up of {aeliss}'s face, a single tear on her cheek, calm voice, sunset light]`
Where is she from? "From a world that fell, master," she says quietly. "It fell on its twenty-eighth day."

**81** `[image: {yunjae} staring at {aeliss}, stunned, the meaning sinking in]`
Fallen worlds. Citizens. Our MC is sharing a raft with the survivor of a world that lost this exact game.

**82** `[image: {aeliss} kneeling at the edge of {raft1} and touching the water with her staff, aqua light rippling outward across the ocean]`
He asks what she can do. She doesn't explain. She just kneels and touches the water with her staff, and light ripples out across the waves.

**83** `[image: a huge silver school of fish swirling up around {raft1} in the clear turquoise water, a faint glow visible far below in the deep water]`
And the sea answers. Fish rise around the raft by the hundreds. Somewhere far below them, something faintly glows. She notices it. She doesn't mention it. Not yet.

**84** `[image: {yunjae} and {aeliss} sitting on {raft1} at dusk eating grilled fish, quiet and calm, the sea golden around them]`
They eat grilled fish as the sun goes down, mostly in silence. When he asks what she was, back home, she answers: "The one who called the tides for my city." Then nothing more.

**85** `[image: close-up of {aeliss}'s hand on her glowing coral staff, the pearl pulsing aqua, determined expression]`
Later, he asks the question that's been bothering him. What happens to her if he dies? She tells him the truth, in the same quiet voice. Citizens go free. Free, and alone, on an ocean with no one.

**86** `[image: night falls over the ocean, a giant glowing holographic leaderboard appearing across the entire starry sky, abstract glowing rows, no readable text]`
At midnight, the first daily ranking appears. Not on a screen. Across the whole sky, for every survivor to see.

**87** `[image: close-up of the top row of the giant leaderboard in the sky blazing gold, abstract glowing symbols, no readable text]`
Number one in the world: Seo Yun-jae. Achievement: first Epic citizen summoned in any zone.

**88** `[image: {yunjae} sitting on {raft1} at night looking up at his name burning gold in the sky, {aeliss} beside him, stars everywhere]`
Our MC looks up at his own name in the sky. Four billion people are looking at it too.

**89** `[image: dozens of small glowing chat windows exploding into the night sky like fireworks, abstract symbols, no readable text]`
Zone chat explodes. Who is Seo Yun-jae? What's his ability? Where is he? That last question makes him a little uneasy.

## The first night

**99** `[image: {raft1} at night on a black ocean under a sky full of stars, a huge dark fin cutting through the water toward it, ominous]`
He doesn't get much sleep. Around two in the morning, something bumps the raft from underneath.

**100** `[image: close-up of {yunjae} jolting awake on {raft1} at night, gripping the edge, eyes wide, the dark water rippling]`
Then it bumps again. Harder. The raft tilts, and seawater washes over the edge.

**101** `[image: a gigantic shark-like sea monster with glowing white eyes rising beside {raft1} in the moonlight, jaws open, terrifying scale]`
In the moonlight, a shape surfaces next to them. It's longer than a bus. And it's been circling the light.

**102** `[image: {aeliss} standing at the edge of {raft1} at night, raising her coral staff, the aqua pearl blazing, the sea around her glowing]`
Aeliss is already standing. She lifts her staff, and the pearl blazes like a small moon.

**103** `[image: a spear of glowing water erupting from the ocean and striking the giant sea monster, spray and moonlight everywhere, dramatic action]`
The sea itself rises up and strikes. A spear of water slams into the creature's side.

**104** `[image: the giant sea monster diving away into the black water, a huge splash, {raft1} rocking in the waves]`
It thrashes once, and dives. The ocean goes quiet again.

**105** `[image: {aeliss} lowering her staff and breathing hard on {raft1} at night, {yunjae} staring at her in shock]`
Our MC stares at her. When he finally asks what that was, she answers like it's obvious. "It wanted the light. Your raft was glowing. I asked it to leave."

**106** `[image: close-up of {panel} glowing in the dark, abstract symbols, no readable text]`
Then the System pops up with a number nobody wanted. "Zone report, night one. Survivors lost: two hundred and fourteen."

**107** `[image: dark ocean at night with a few broken empty rafts drifting, scattered planks floating, no people, somber]`
Two hundred and fourteen people in his zone didn't see the sun come up. Some fell asleep and rolled off. Some met whatever just met him.

**108** `[image: close-up of {yunjae}'s face at night, jaw tight, looking at the dark water]`
Our MC doesn't sleep after that. He sits up and watches the water until the sky turns gray.

## Day two

**109** `[image: sunrise over an endless calm ocean, golden light spreading across the water, {raft1} small in the frame]`

**110** `[image: a small glowing white chest appearing on {raft1} in the morning light, a soft burst of white light]`

**111** `[image: a rolled-up glowing parchment blueprint sketch of a mast and sail floating above an open white chest, abstract lines, no readable text]`

**112** `[image: {yunjae} sitting on {raft1} studying a glowing blueprint, {aeliss} looking over his shoulder curiously, morning light]`

**115** `[image: a man in a suit on {raft} holding up a glowing white card proudly, other rafts far away in the background]`

**116** `[image: {taegu} standing on {raft} flexing, a pile of planks beside him, smug grin, the ocean behind him]`

**117** `[image: close-up of a glowing chat window with a silhouette avatar of a muscular man, abstract symbols, no readable text]`

**119** `[image: a giant glowing globe of light floating over the ocean with thousands of tiny chat windows orbiting it, abstract, no readable text]`

**120** `[image: a woman with short blonde hair on a raft far away, a stocky bearded dwarf citizen with a hammer standing proudly beside her, surprised smile]`
