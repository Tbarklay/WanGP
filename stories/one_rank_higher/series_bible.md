# ONE RANK HIGHER: Series Bible

**Long title for YouTube:** Everyone Was Thrown Into Another World to Survive on a Raft — But Only I Can Upgrade Everything

**Logline:** At midnight, everyone on Earth aged 18 to 65 wakes up alone on a tiny raft in an endless ocean, with a
System that talks. A quiet repairman gets an ability that can push anything one rank higher, and from the very first
day, he's number one in the world.

## Release plan
- **8 episodes per season**, each **20-30 minutes** (about 3,000-4,000 narrated words, 150-200 illustrations).
- Each season is one 28-day cycle and ends on a World Battle.
- **No background music.** Narration only (see stories/CLAUDE.md).
- Recap style: the narration calls the hero "our MC"; his name appears in System messages and rankings.

## The Trial (world rules)
1. **The Summoning:** at midnight, everyone aged 18-65 vanishes from Earth. Everyone younger or older stays behind.
2. **The Drift:** an endless ocean in another dimension. Everyone starts on an identical **2x2 raft**, with a
   fishing rod, three spare planks and one day of water.
3. **Zones:** 10,000 people per zone, each starting **50 km apart**. Meeting someone happens by chance: rafts
   drifting into each other, smoke or islands on the horizon. When a zone is down to **1,000** survivors, **10 zones
   merge** into a new zone of 10,000.
4. **The System:** everyone gets one, and it talks. Each System has its own personality (our MC's is dry,
   sarcastic and secretly protective). Daily login rewards, rankings, zone chat and world chat.
5. **Abilities:** everyone starts with one random ability that matches their personality (the hoarder gets
   Infinite Pocket, the gym bro Iron Body, the nurse Mend, the coward Vanish).
6. **Drops:** chests from fishing, wreckage and events, and later from mining. Chest rarity follows the colour tiers.
7. **Citizen cards:** summon people from **fallen worlds** (worlds that lost the Trial): humans, elves, dwarves,
   beastkin and more. Once activated, a citizen works, builds, fights and levels up, and has a loyalty score.
8. **Rarity tiers:** ⚪ White (Common), 🟢 Green (Uncommon), 🔵 Blue (Rare), 🟣 Purple (Epic),
   🟡 Gold (Legendary), 🔴 Red (Apex).
9. **Rankings:** daily, event and battle rankings, global. Rewards for the top places.
10. **World Events:** on days 7, 14 and 21 (and every week after): endless rain, deep freeze, fog with blind
    hunters, heat wave, sea monster attacks and more. Points for how well you prepared and how well you survived.
11. **World Battle:** every 28 days, Earth's survivors fight another world's survivors. **If Earth loses, Earth
    falls**, and its people become citizen cards in someone else's deck.
12. **Hints:** 24 hours before each event or battle, the System gives a riddle with three layers: what's coming, a
    hidden clue to surviving it, and a plausible wrong reading that splits the chat.
13. **Island Cores:** nobody has land at the start. The first Cores are rewards for the top 100 of each zone in the
    Day 28 World Battle (#1 Gold, #2-10 Purple, #11-30 Blue, #31-60 Green, #61-100 White). Throw a Core into the
    water and an island rises there. Feed more Cores into it to make it bigger. Allies can **Neighbor**: plant
    their islands next to yours and link them.
14. **Dead islands:** a handful (3-5) per zone, left over from a fallen world, hidden and hard to find. A
    **Wayfinder Map** from a purple-or-better fishing chest points to the nearest one. Claiming its Core makes the
    island **collapse into the ocean**: you have to race back to your raft before it takes you down. The Core can
    then start an island anywhere or upgrade the one you have.
15. **Death and loot:** when someone dies, their killer gets their whole inventory: unused Cores, resources and
    unactivated citizen cards. Their activated citizens go free. If they owned an island, it starts collapsing,
    with its Core inside: get to it before it sinks.

## Our MC: Seo Yun-jae, 24
- **Before:** a repair technician in Seoul who fixed everyone's broken things and never got credit. Quiet,
  patient, always sees how something could be better. Lives with his grandmother (72) and his little sister
  Ha-eun (15), who were both left behind on Earth. Getting back to them is his reason to fight.
- **Ability: [Refine] → [Ascend] → [Transcend]**
  - **[Refine]** (levels 1-4): raises any item, card, chest or building one rank, with a small luck chance of
    two. 3 uses a day. Each thing can only be refined once.
  - **[Ascend]** (levels 5-9): a much higher chance of +2, more uses a day.
  - **[Transcend]** (max level, late reveal): can push a Red Apex thing past Red, into a rank the System has no
    colour for.
  - Works on unopened chests: a refined chest rolls on a better loot table.
- **Always #1 in the world ranking.**

## Main cast
- **Aeliss** (🟣 Epic citizen, from episode 1): an elf tide-caller from a fallen world of silver shores. Calls fish
  and currents, senses what lies under the water. Calm, sad, quietly fierce. Her world fell on its 28th day.
- **Kaiser** (#2 in the world): an ex-CEO who has never come second at anything. Ability **[Conquest]**: when he
  kills someone, their activated citizens become his, loyal or not. That's why he wants our MC dead: one kill would
  give him the strongest citizens in the Drift. Starts in a different zone; they first meet at the Day 28 World
  Battle, and each merge brings his zone closer.

- **Ostrel** (🔵 Blue citizen, Kaiser's first): a silent knight from a fallen kingdom, steel-blue armour, a helmet that
  never comes off. Kaiser's right hand and bodyguard; at the Day 28 World Battle, he's the blade Kaiser sends at our
  MC's back. Whether he wants to is a question for later.

- **Yoon Da-hye** (Earth survivor, ability **[Mend]**): an ER nurse who keeps her head while zone chat panics. Seen
  for a moment in episode 1; later his first real ally and **Neighbor**, the one Earth human he trusts. Not a
  citizen, so Kaiser's Conquest can't take her, which matters later.

- **Bang Tae-gu** (Earth survivor, ability **[Iron Body]**): the gym guy flexing in zone chat in episode 1. By
  episode 3 he's gathered a crew of rafts and become the zone's first raider boss, and he comes for our MC's raft
  after the purple pillar of light gives its location away. His Iron Body makes him nearly impossible to hurt,
  which forces our MC to win with his head.

## Citizen status and affinity
- Our MC can open a **status window** for every citizen: **Health, Strength, Agility, Mana** (more stats unlock
  later), plus **Loyalty** (how bound they are to him; summoned citizens start around 60%) and **Affinity** (how
  they feel about him personally; shown only for citizens of the opposite sex, starts near 5%).
- On screen the windows are glowing coloured bars with no readable text; the narration reads the numbers.
- Once a citizen's Affinity passes **80%**, the two of them can become intimate, always by her choice and
  consensual, and only between adults. In the videos this stays **off-screen**: a fade to black or the narrator
  moving on, never shown. It keeps the channel YouTube-safe.

## The women of his island (slow-burn tension)
Our MC collects strong women citizens over the series, and each one has her own kind of tension with him. Keep it
YouTube-safe: charged banter, close calls, jealousy, glances that last a beat too long, loyalty turning into
something more. Never explicit. Every new arrival shifts the balance between the others.

- **Aeliss** (🟣 Epic → later refined higher; elf tide-caller, episode 1): quiet and mysterious. Her first words
  are "Hello, master." She answers anything he asks, honestly, but never says anything he didn't ask for, until
  she starts to trust him (her first unasked words come at the end of day 2). The calm, sad one. The tension starts on a
  2x2 raft where there's nowhere to look but each other. She keeps her distance because she has lost everything once,
  and he is the first thing she's afraid to lose. Slowest burn, deepest bond.
- **Torva Emberhand** (🔵 Blue refined to 🟡 Gold; dwarf master smith, around episode 8): blunt, loud, competitive,
  and offended that a human's skill can "fix" her work. Constant teasing and challenges ("refine THIS"); she's the
  one who flirts openly, and the others hate it.
- **Rhea Ironfang** (🟢 Green refined to 🟣 Epic; wolf-kin warrior captain, around episode 12): fierce and
  protective, tests him in sparring and won't follow a weak leader. Tension through rivalry: she pins him, he
  doesn't back down, and something changes.
- **Kaela of the Ember Dunes** (enemy at the Day 28 World Battle): a fire-beastkin fighter from the world that has
  "never known water". Enemies to allies: she spares him once, he spares her once, and when her world falls she
  appears as a card in his hand. The most dangerous tension: she should hate him.
- **Celestine Varr** (🔴 Red Apex, a late-season pull): the last empress of a fallen human empire, proud and
  certain he's beneath her. Her loyalty score starts low and is the hardest one in the series to earn.

How the tension plays on screen:
- The **loyalty score** has a hidden second bar the System calls "affection". The System comments on it, dryly.
- Every new citizen rattles the others: who stands next to him, who sleeps closest on the raft, who he refines first.
- Kaiser's Conquest makes it personal: if he kills our MC, these women become his. That gives every fight with Kaiser
  higher stakes.

## Season 1 outline (8 episodes, 20-30 minutes each)
1. **The Raft** (days 1-2) — the Summoning, the System, [Refine], a green card refined to purple (Aeliss), #1 in the
   world; the first night's sea monster; world chat and Kaiser's "company"; Aeliss's fallen world; the refined purple
   chest, the first sailing raft (level 2), #1 again; Tae-gu's three rafts come for him.
2. **Raiders** (days 2-6) — the fight with Tae-gu's crew; the first kill and the loot rule; the first riddle.
3. **The Endless Rain** (day 7 and after) — the shelter raft (level 3) rides out the rain; a refined chest gives the
   Wayfinder Map.
4. **The Dead Island** (days 9-13) — Torva the dwarf smith; the ruins, the Core, the collapse escape; the first
   island in the zone.
5. **Neighbors** (day 14 and after) — the fog with blind hunters; Da-hye becomes his first Neighbor.
6. **Teeth of the Sea** (days 17-21) — Rhea the wolf-kin warrior; the sea monster attack.
7. **The Last Riddle** (days 22-27) — the World Battle hint; Earth meets face to face; alliances, grudges, Kaiser's plan.
8. **World Battle** (day 28) — the fire world, Kaiser and Ostrel's move, Kaela, the Gold Core refined to Red, cliffhanger.

## The raft, level by level
The first refined green plank stays in every version, a piece of continuity viewers can spot. Cast keys:
- `{raft}` the starter, four plain planks: everyone's raft, and our MC's before the refine
- `{raft1}` the starter with the glowing green plank (episode 1 after the refine)
- `{raft2}` 3x3 m, small mast and patched sail, crate, rod holder (episode 2)
- `{raft3}` 4x4 m log frame, roof shelter, rain barrels, stove (episodes 4-5, the rain)
- `{raft4}` twin-hull catamaran with a cabin (around episode 8)
- `{raft5}` armored sailing ship (around episode 12, until the island)

**Everyone else sails the same models, without the green plank.** Every survivor's raft or boat uses the plain
version of the same level (`{raft}` for the starter, then `{raft2_plain}` ... `{raft5_plain}`), so viewers can compare at
a glance how far ahead our MC is. Other survivors are usually one or two levels behind him: when he has the
catamaran, the strongest rivals have the shelter raft and most of the zone is still on the starter. Only our MC's
version ever has the glowing plank. All plain references exist (`refs/raft2_plain.png` ...
`refs/raft5_plain.png`), cropped from the same view as our MC's versions so they match exactly.

> high contrast, vivid saturated colors, detailed background, 16:9 widescreen composition
