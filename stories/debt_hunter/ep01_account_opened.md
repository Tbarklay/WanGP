# The Debt Hunter, Episode 1: "Account Opened"

Target length: 10-12 minutes (about 1,650 narrated words at a steady storytelling pace).
Each beat is one illustration, shown while its narration plays. `{cass}`, `{ledger}` and the other names in braces
are filled in with the matching description from `series_bible.md`, so every image draws the characters the same way.

---

## Cold open

**01** `[image: {cass} on his knees in a collapsing cave, red light pouring through cracks in the walls, rocks falling, low angle]`
Twelve minutes ago, Cass Arden was the most useless person in this rift.

**02** `[image: extreme close-up of {cass}'s face, blood on his cheek, gray eyes wide, crimson light reflected in them]`
Now he's the only one still in it. Bleeding, out of time, and about to make a deal with something he doesn't understand.

**03** `[image: {ledger} floating open in front of {cass} in the dark cave, crimson light spilling from its pages]`
A book that shouldn't exist has just opened in front of him. And it's asking him a question.

**04** `[image: black screen with a single thin crimson crack of light down the middle]`
But to understand how he got here, we have to go back to this morning.

## The world

**05** `[image: wide shot of a modern coastal city of glass towers at dawn, a giant glowing crack splitting the sky above it]`
Seven years ago, the sky over Harrowgate cracked open. Nobody knows why. One morning there was just a line of light across the clouds, like someone had dropped the world and it chipped.

**06** `[image: monsters pouring out of a sky rift onto a city street, people running, cars overturned, dramatic chaos]`
Then the cracks started opening. And things came out of them.

**07** `[image: inside a rift, a vast cavern lit by floating blue crystals, strange ruins and alien plants]`
People started calling them rifts: pockets of another world, full of monsters and treasure. Leave one alone for seven days, and it collapses, and everything inside spills out into the streets.

**08** `[image: close-up of a hand with a glowing orange mark on the back, energy swirling around it]`
But the cracks brought something else, too. Some people woke up with a mark on the back of their hand, a glowing Crest, and with it came power.

**09** `[image: five glowing hand Crests side by side, gray, orange, white, yellow and violet]`
Iron. Bronze. Silver. Gold. And at the very top, Crown. Your Crest decides what you are. And it never, ever changes.

**10** `[image: heroic hunters with glowing Crests posing for cameras on a city rooftop, fans and drones around them]`
The people with Crests became hunters. They clear rifts, save cities, and get paid like movie stars.

**11** `[image: a line of tired workers with huge backpacks and no Crests, waiting outside a rift gate in the rain]`
The people without Crests? Some of them became porters. They carry the gear, the food and the loot, so the hunters don't have to. They're cheap, replaceable, and paid by the trip.

## Cass

**12** `[image: {cass} waking up in a tiny cramped apartment, early morning light through a cracked window, an alarm clock on the floor]`
Cass Arden is nineteen years old. He's been a porter for three years.

**13** `[image: a cluttered desk with stacks of overdue bills stamped IRONVALE GUILD, a framed photo of a smiling man]`
Not because he loves it. Because when his father died, he left Cass two things: an old photo, and a debt to the Ironvale Guild so big it has its own interest rate.

**14** `[image: {cass} counting a few crumpled bills at his kitchen table, an empty fridge open behind him]`
Every trip into a rift pays a little. Every trip, the debt grows a little more anyway. Cass has done the math. At this rate, he'll pay it off when he's sixty-three.

**15** `[image: close-up of {cass}'s worn pocket notebook, pages full of neat notes and sketches of hunters and monsters]`
But Cass has one habit nobody else bothers with. He takes notes. Every hunter he's worked with, every skill he's seen, every monster's attack pattern, all written down in a battered little notebook.

**16** `[image: {cass} walking through rainy city streets under the glowing sky crack, notebook in his jacket pocket]`
Hunters think it's weird. Porters think it's pointless. Cass thinks it's the only thing keeping him alive.

## The guild

**17** `[image: a towering black glass skyscraper with a giant iron crest logo, IRONVALE written on the side]`
His first stop today is the place he hates most in the city: Ironvale Guild headquarters.

**18** `[image: luxurious guild lobby with marble floors and trophy cases of monster horns, {cass} looking small at the front desk]`
The lobby is marble, glass and monster trophies. Every horn on the wall is worth more than Cass makes in a year.

**19** `[image: {hale} descending a grand staircase, leaning on his crystal-handled cane, smiling coldly at {cass}]`
And waiting at the top of the stairs is the man who owns his father's debt: Director Silas Hale.

**20** `[image: {hale} close-up, polite smile that doesn't reach his eyes]`
"Mr. Arden. Your payment was light this month." He says it like he's talking about the weather. He always does.

**21** `[image: {cass} clenching his fist at his side, eyes lowered]`
Cass tells him it'll be better next month. He's said it so many times, the words don't even taste like anything anymore.

**22** `[image: {hale} handing {cass} a contract on a tablet, glowing rift map on the screen]`
Hale slides him a contract. An Iron-rank rift on the east side. Low danger. One team. Easy money. "Try not to drop anything expensive," he says.

## The team

**23** `[image: a small rift gate glowing blue inside a fenced construction site, police tape and floodlights]`
The rift is sitting inside a fenced-off construction site, a crack in the air about the size of a garage door, humming like a broken power line.

**24** `[image: {pike} posing confidently in front of the rift, cape fluttering, {bram} and {lise} beside him]`
The team is already waiting. Dorian Pike, Bronze-rank, team leader, and someone who has clearly practiced that cape flip in a mirror.

**25** `[image: {bram} sneering and dropping a huge bag of gear at {cass}'s feet]`
Bram, the archer, doesn't say hello. He just drops forty kilos of gear at Cass's feet. "Try to keep up, pack mule."

**26** `[image: {lise} giving {cass} a small apologetic smile and a water bottle]`
Lise, the healer, is the only one who looks at Cass like he's a person. She hands him a water bottle when the others aren't looking.

**27** `[image: {cass} writing in his notebook while watching the team, glowing rift behind them]`
Cass writes down everything. Pike: fire-coated sword strikes, three-second wind-up. Bram: arrows that split in two. Lise: a healing light that closes small cuts. Old habits.

**28** `[image: the team stepping through the glowing rift gate, {cass} last, bent under his huge pack]`
Then they step through.

## Inside the rift

**29** `[image: inside the rift, a huge cavern of floating blue crystals and rock bridges over a glowing abyss]`
Inside, the rift is a cavern the size of a stadium, lit by blue crystals floating in the air like lanterns.

**30** `[image: {pike} slashing a small rock lizard monster with a flaming sword, sparks flying]`
The first hour is exactly what the contract promised. Iron-rank monsters, rock lizards and cave bats. Pike cuts through them like he's filming a commercial.

**31** `[image: {cass} kneeling to collect glowing crystal shards from a defeated monster, gear piled on his back]`
Cass collects the loot: glowing shards called rift energy. It's what hunters sell, what guilds trade, and what keeps the lights on in Harrowgate.

**32** `[image: {cass} frowning, looking at a hairline crack of red light in the cavern wall]`
And then Cass notices something nobody else does. A thin red crack in the cavern wall. Rifts are supposed to glow blue.

**33** `[image: {cass} checking his notebook, a page with a sketch of a red crack and the words underlined]`
He's seen that red once before, in his notes from two years ago. Underlined three times. The note says: "Rift collapse warning."

**34** `[image: {cass} calling out to {pike}, who waves him off without turning around]`
He tells Pike. Pike laughs. "It's an Iron-rank rift, porter. It's not going to collapse on day one."

**35** `[image: the red crack spreading across the cavern wall like lightning]`
The crack spreads.

## The collapse

**36** `[image: the whole cavern shaking, crystals falling from the ceiling, red light bursting through the walls]`
The ground lurches. Crystals fall from the ceiling like glass rain. The blue light turns red, all at once, everywhere.

**37** `[image: a huge armored monster silhouette rising behind a wall of dust, glowing orange cracks in its body]`
And from somewhere deeper in the rift, something big starts walking.

**38** `[image: {warden} emerging from the dust, stone plates, molten orange eyes, horns, roaring]`
It's covered in layered stone plates, with fire glowing between the cracks. Cass has read about these. A Gravel Warden. A Silver-rank monster. In an Iron-rank rift.

**39** `[image: {pike} slashing at {warden}, his flaming sword shattering against its stone armor]`
Pike swings anyway. His sword hits the Warden's shoulder and shatters like a toy.

**40** `[image: {bram}'s split arrows bouncing harmlessly off {warden}'s armor]`
Bram's arrows bounce off. Lise's light doesn't even reach it. In three seconds, the confident team leader is gone and a terrified kid is in his place.

**41** `[image: {pike} screaming "run" and sprinting toward the glowing exit gate, the team following]`
"Run!" Pike screams. And they run.

**42** `[image: {cass} struggling under his massive pack, falling behind the running team on a crumbling rock bridge]`
Cass runs too. But he's carrying forty kilos of their gear, and the rock bridge is falling apart under his feet.

## The betrayal

**43** `[image: {cass} reaching for the exit gate, {pike} turning back toward him]`
He gets close. The exit gate is right there. Pike is standing in it, looking back at him.

**44** `[image: {pike}'s cold face in the gate light, his hand on the gate controls]`
For one second, Cass thinks he's going to wait.

**45** `[image: {pike} shoving {cass} backward off the bridge edge, {lise} screaming in the background]`
Instead, Pike shoves him. Hard. Back toward the monster. "Slow it down, pack mule. That's what we pay you for."

**46** `[image: {lise} reaching out toward {cass} as {bram} drags her through the gate]`
Lise screams his name. Bram drags her through the gate.

**47** `[image: the exit gate flickering and shrinking into a tiny point of light, {cass} alone in the darkness]`
And then the gate shrinks to a point of light, and it's gone.

## Alone

**48** `[image: {cass} lying among rubble, his backpack torn open, crystals scattered around him]`
Cass lands hard in the rubble. His pack splits open. Something in his leg makes a sound he really doesn't like.

**49** `[image: {warden}'s massive stone foot slamming down right next to {cass}'s head]`
The Gravel Warden doesn't hurry. It doesn't need to. Every step shakes the whole cavern.

**50** `[image: {cass} crawling backward against a wall, notebook falling out of his jacket, monster looming]`
Cass crawls backward until there's a wall behind him. His notebook falls out of his jacket and lands open on the ground.

**51** `[image: close-up of the open notebook, a page titled GRAVEL WARDEN with sketches of its stone plates]`
It's open to a page he wrote two years ago. Gravel Warden. Weak point: none recorded. Recommended response: do not engage.

**52** `[image: {cass} laughing weakly, blood on his lip, the monster's glowing eyes above him]`
He actually laughs. Three years of notes, and the best advice he ever wrote down is "don't."

**53** `[image: {cass}'s face in close-up, eyes closed, a faded memory image of his smiling father behind him]`
He thinks about his father. About the debt. About how Director Hale will probably add a fee for the lost equipment.

## The Ledger

**54** `[image: the notebook's pages flipping wildly on their own, crimson light leaking from between them]`
And then the notebook starts to move.

**55** `[image: the notebook transforming into {ledger}, black leather and brass corners growing over it, floating up]`
Its pages flip on their own. The cheap cover turns to black leather. Brass corners grow over it like frost. And it rises into the air in front of him.

**56** `[image: {ledger} open in midair, glowing crimson handwriting appearing on a blank page]`
Words write themselves across the page in glowing red ink.

**57** `[image: close-up of the glowing page with elegant crimson handwriting, no readable text, just glowing script]`
"Account holder detected. Observation records found: three hundred and twelve. Account opened."

**58** `[image: {cass} staring at {ledger} in disbelief, its crimson light on his face]`
Cass stares at it. A floating book, in a collapsing rift, giving him a bank statement.

**59** `[image: {ledger}'s page showing a glowing sketch of {warden}, armor plates outlined in crimson]`
The page turns. There's a drawing of the Gravel Warden, his drawing from two years ago, but now it glows. Underneath, a new line: "Ability recorded: Stoneplate Body."

**60** `[image: {ledger} glowing brighter, a crimson question mark of light hovering above the page]`
And then the question. "Would you like to borrow?"

**61** `[image: {warden} raising both front legs high above {cass}, about to slam down]`
The Warden rears up above him. Cass doesn't have time to read the fine print.

**62** `[image: {cass} slamming his hand onto the glowing page of {ledger}, crimson light exploding outward]`
He slams his hand on the page. "Yes."

## The fight

**63** `[image: stone plates with glowing orange cracks forming over {cass}'s arms and shoulders like armor]`
Stone plates burst across his skin, layer over layer, orange fire glowing in the cracks. The same armor the monster has. Every bit of it.

**64** `[image: {warden}'s huge feet slamming down onto {cass}, who catches them on his armored arms, shockwave of dust]`
The Warden's legs come down, and Cass catches them. Both of them. The ground cracks beneath him, but he doesn't move an inch.

**65** `[image: a glowing crimson countdown floating at the edge of the frame, the number ten in elegant script]`
In the corner of his vision, a number glows red. Ten. Nine.

**66** `[image: {cass} shoving {warden} backward, the monster stumbling, shocked]`
He shoves. A Silver-rank monster stumbles backward like it's been hit by a truck.

**67** `[image: {cass} charging forward, fist glowing with orange cracks, low dramatic angle]`
Eight. Seven. Cass doesn't have a weapon. He doesn't need one.

**68** `[image: {cass}'s armored fist smashing into {warden}'s chest plate, cracks spreading through the stone]`
He punches it in the chest. Stone hits stone, and the monster's armor cracks first.

**69** `[image: {cass} hitting again and again, fragments of stone flying, crimson countdown at three]`
Six. Five. Four. Again. Again. Every hit is borrowed strength, and he spends it all.

**70** `[image: {warden} shattering into pieces, glowing orange fragments exploding outward, {cass} standing in the light]`
Three. Two. On the last hit, the Gravel Warden breaks apart into a storm of glowing stone.

**71** `[image: the stone armor crumbling off {cass} as he drops to his knees, exhausted, crystals falling around him]`
One. The armor crumbles off him like dry clay, and Cass falls to his knees in the silence.

## The bill

**72** `[image: a huge glowing rift-energy crystal rising from the monster's remains, lighting up the cavern]`
Where the Warden stood, a rift-energy crystal the size of a watermelon is glowing. It's worth more than a year of porter jobs.

**73** `[image: the cavern's red light fading back to calm blue, a new exit gate opening behind {cass}]`
The red light fades. The rift goes still. And behind him, a new exit gate opens.

**74** `[image: {cass} limping out of the rift gate into floodlights, police and guild workers turning in shock]`
Cass Arden limps out of a collapsed rift, alone, carrying a Silver-rank crystal. As far as anyone knows, no porter has ever done that.

**75** `[image: {pike} and {bram} staring at {cass} in shock and fear from across the construction site]`
Pike and Bram are standing by the guild truck. They look at him like they've seen a ghost. Which, from their point of view, they kind of have.

**76** `[image: {lise} running toward {cass} with tears in her eyes]`
Lise is the only one who runs toward him.

**77** `[image: {ledger} floating unseen beside {cass}, crimson light, nobody else noticing it]`
Nobody else can see the book floating at his shoulder. Cass realizes that a second later, when nobody screams.

**78** `[image: {ledger} open to a page where crimson ink spreads into a long ledger entry with a glowing seal]`
The Ledger opens one last time tonight. New line. "Loan: Stoneplate Body, ten seconds. Debt: one thousand rift energy."

**79** `[image: close-up of glowing crimson script and a small glowing hourglass on the page]`
"Due in seventy-two hours. Interest applies."

**80** `[image: {cass} looking down at the huge crystal in his arms, then at the book, realizing]`
Cass looks at the crystal in his arms. Worth maybe four hundred. Then at the book. One thousand. Seventy-two hours.

**81** `[image: night rooftop far away, {auditor} standing in silhouette watching the construction site, pocket watch open]`
And on a rooftop across the street, a figure in a long black coat closes a brass pocket watch, and writes something down.

**82** `[image: {cass} under the cracked glowing sky, the Ledger's crimson light on his face, determined expression]`
Cass Arden just borrowed his way out of death. Now he has three days to pay for it.

**83** `[image: title card style image of {ledger} glowing in the dark with crimson light, dramatic and ominous]`
Next time on The Debt Hunter: Interest.
