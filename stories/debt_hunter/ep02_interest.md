# The Debt Hunter, Episode 2: "Interest"

Target length: about 10-11 minutes (about 1,500 narrated words at a steady storytelling pace).
Each beat is one illustration, shown while its narration plays. `{cass}`, `{ledger}` and the other names in braces
are filled in with the matching description from `series_bible.md`, so every image draws the characters the same way.
The narration calls the hero "our MC" or "the main character", recap style; his name, Cass Arden, stays in the bible and image prompts.
Numbers in the narration are written as words so the narrator reads them naturally.

---

## Cold open

**01** `[image: {cass} running through a dark flooded subway tunnel lit by glowing purple crystals, swinging a rusty iron pipe wreathed in orange flames, low angle]`
Our MC is sprinting through an abandoned subway tunnel, swinging a rusty pipe that is somehow on fire.

**02** `[image: close-up of {ledger} floating beside {cass} in the dark, a glowing crimson hourglass on its page with almost no sand left]`
Floating next to him, the Ledger is showing a countdown. Fifty-nine minutes left to pay a debt he can't afford.

**03** `[image: dozens of glowing eyes in the darkness of a subway tunnel, the silhouettes of rats with crystal spikes on their backs]`
And behind him, something with a lot of glowing eyes is catching up.

**04** `[image: pure black background with a thin glowing crimson line drawn across the middle like a signature, no people]`
But to understand how a porter ended up here, we have to go back three days.

## The morning after

**05** `[image: {cass} asleep face down on a small bed in a tiny cramped apartment, still wearing his jacket, morning light through a cracked window, {ledger} floating above the bed]`
The morning after the rift, our MC wakes up in his tiny apartment, still in yesterday's clothes. For one perfect second, he thinks it was all a dream.

**06** `[image: close-up of {ledger} floating in midair above the bed, crimson light glowing between its pages]`
Then he sees the book. Floating over his bed. Glowing. Very much not a dream.

**07** `[image: close-up of {ledger}'s open page with a glowing crimson hourglass and rows of glowing red figures like a bank statement, no readable text]`
The page shows exactly what he owes. One thousand rift energy, due in sixty-eight hours. The hourglass on the page is already running.

**08** `[image: {cass} sitting on the edge of his bed holding a huge glowing blue crystal in both hands, looking at it nervously]`
He still has the crystal from the Gravel Warden. A guild appraiser would pay good money for it. But the Ledger doesn't want money.

**09** `[image: {cass} holding the glowing blue crystal over the open pages of {ledger}, blue light being pulled into the crimson pages like water draining]`
On a hunch, he holds the crystal over the open page. The book drinks it. The blue light pours into the pages like water down a drain.

**10** `[image: close-up of {ledger}'s page with a glowing crimson stamp and a short column of glowing red figures, no readable text]`
New line. Payment received: four hundred and twelve. Remaining debt: five hundred and eighty-eight.

**11** `[image: {cass} lying back on his bed staring at the ceiling, an empty hand where the crystal was, exhausted expression]`
So the rule is simple. The Ledger only takes rift energy. And our MC just fed it a year's worth of rent.

## The guild

**12** `[image: a big flat screen in a cafe showing a news broadcast about a collapsed rift at a construction site, police tape and floodlights on the screen]`
On the way to work, every screen in the city is playing the same story. A rift collapsed on day one. And a porter walked out alive.

**13** `[image: {cass} walking through the marble lobby of a guild headquarters while hunters in armor turn their heads to stare at him]`
At Ironvale Guild, the hunters stare at him like he's a ghost. The receptionist actually drops her coffee.

**14** `[image: {hale} standing at the top of a grand staircase, leaning on his crystal-handled cane, thin smile that doesn't reach his eyes]`
And of course, Director Hale is waiting at the top of the stairs.

**15** `[image: {hale} sitting behind a huge dark wooden desk in a luxurious office with a view over the city, {cass} standing across from him]`
In his office, Hale congratulates him for surviving. Then he slides a piece of paper across the desk.

**16** `[image: close-up of a glossy paper bill on a dark desk, a red stamped seal, a gloved hand resting next to it, no readable text]`
It's a bill. For forty kilos of lost guild equipment. Added straight onto his father's debt.

**17** `[image: close-up of {hale}'s cold pale blue eyes, the glowing blue crystal of his cane reflected in them]`
Then Hale asks, very casually, where exactly the Silver-rank crystal went. The guild found the empty spot where the Warden died.

**18** `[image: {cass} forcing a nervous smile in front of {hale}'s desk, a bead of sweat on his temple]`
Our MC says he dropped it while running. Hale smiles. It is very clear that he doesn't believe a single word.

**19** `[image: {cass} walking down a guild hallway, a glowing red notice on a tablet in his hand, shoulders slumped]`
On the way out, he gets the real news. He's suspended from porter work while the guild investigates. No rifts. No pay.

## A visitor

**20** `[image: {cass} sitting on the steps outside his apartment building at dusk, holding his head in his hands, city lights coming on]`
Five hundred and eighty-eight rift energy. Sixty hours. No job. Our MC sits on the steps of his building and does the math again. It does not get better.

**21** `[image: {lise} walking up to the apartment steps at dusk holding a paper bag of food, nervous apologetic expression]`
Then someone sits down next to him. It's Lise, the healer from the team that left him behind.

**22** `[image: {lise} and {cass} sitting side by side on the steps, Lise holding out a paper bag of food, guilt on her face]`
She brought food, and an apology. "Pike told the guild you panicked and ran off on your own," she says. "I know that isn't true."

**23** `[image: {lise} looking down at her hands, worried, {cass} listening beside her]`
She also brings something more useful. There's a tiny rift in an old subway tunnel downtown. Iron rank. The guild never bothered with it, because clearing it isn't worth the paperwork.

**24** `[image: close-up of {lise} looking at {cass} seriously, streetlight on her face]`
"Nobody's watching that one," she says. "I'm just telling you. I'm not telling you to go." She is absolutely telling him to go.

## Reading the rules

**25** `[image: {cass} at a small kitchen table late at night, {ledger} open in front of him under a desk lamp, his old pocket notebook beside it]`
That night, our MC does what he does best. He studies. Every page of the Ledger, like it's an exam he can't afford to fail.

**26** `[image: close-up of {ledger}'s pages with small glowing crimson sketches of a flaming sword, a split arrow, a glowing green hand and a stone beast, no readable text]`
Every ability he's ever written down is in here. Pike's flaming sword strike. Bram's arrow that splits in two. Lise's healing light. The Warden's stone armor.

**27** `[image: close-up of a glowing crimson price tag symbol next to each sketch on the Ledger's page]`
And every one of them has a price. A small skill costs about a hundred and fifty to borrow. The Warden's armor costs a thousand. Stronger power, bigger loan.

**28** `[image: {cass} leaning back in his chair with a tired grin, rubbing his eyes, {ledger} glowing on the table]`
So the plan is simple. Borrow as little as possible. Earn as much as possible. Basically, the opposite of how his father lived.

**29** `[image: close-up of {cass}'s pocket notebook with a hand-drawn subway tunnel map and small sketches of rats, a pencil next to it]`
He fills three pages with notes on Iron-rank subway monsters. Glass rats. Tunnel moths. What they eat, how they move, where they hide.

## The subway rift

**30** `[image: an abandoned subway station at night, tiled walls covered in graffiti, a small glowing purple crack in the air at the end of the platform]`
At two in the morning, our MC climbs down into the abandoned Line Four subway station. At the end of the platform, a small purple crack hangs in the air.

**31** `[image: {cass} standing in front of the small glowing purple rift crack holding a rusty iron pipe, a flashlight strapped to his shoulder]`
No team. No armor. One flashlight, one rusty pipe, and a magic book that charges interest.

**32** `[image: {cass} stepping through the purple rift into a flooded tunnel lit by clusters of glowing purple crystals growing from the walls]`
He steps through. Inside, the tunnel goes on much further than any tunnel should, lit by purple crystals growing out of the walls.

**33** `[image: a cluster of large rats with sharp glass crystal spikes on their backs, gnawing on a purple crystal, in a dark wet tunnel]`
The first thing he finds is exactly what his notes predicted. Glass rats, about the size of house cats, with crystal spikes on their backs.

**34** `[image: {cass} crouching behind an old rusted subway car, watching the glass rats, his notebook open in his hand]`
And the second thing he notices is something his notes didn't mention. There are a lot more of them than there should be.

**35** `[image: dozens of glass rats turning their heads at once toward the camera, their eyes glowing purple]`
One of them sniffs the air. Then all of them turn around at once.

## The first loan

**36** `[image: the swarm of glass rats charging through the flooded tunnel toward the camera, water splashing, crystal spikes glinting]`
The swarm charges. Our MC has maybe two seconds to decide how much he wants to owe.

**37** `[image: {cass} with one hand on the glowing page of {ledger}, a small glowing sketch of a flaming sword lighting up]`
He picks the cheapest weapon he's got. Pike's flaming sword strike. One hundred and fifty rift energy.

**38** `[image: the rusty iron pipe in {cass}'s hands bursting into bright orange flames, his face lit up in surprise]`
The rusty pipe bursts into flame. Apparently the Ledger doesn't care whether you have a real sword.

**39** `[image: {cass} swinging the flaming iron pipe in a wide arc, a wave of fire sweeping through the charging glass rats]`
He swings, and a wave of fire sweeps through the tunnel. The glass rats scatter, squealing.

**40** `[image: close-up of {cass} grinning in the firelight, holding the burning pipe, sparks floating around him]`
For a second, he feels like a real hunter. Then he remembers that Pike uses this exact move. Which is a little bit insulting.

**41** `[image: the flames on the iron pipe fading out into smoke, a few glass rats still circling in the shadows]`
Ten seconds later, the fire fades. The Ledger adds a new line. Debt: seven hundred and thirty-eight. He's going the wrong way.

## Thinking instead of borrowing

**42** `[image: {cass} backing up against a tunnel wall, the remaining glass rats closing in slowly, his notebook in one hand]`
The rats regroup. He could borrow again. Instead, he flips his notebook open with one hand.

**43** `[image: close-up of {cass}'s notebook page with a sketch of a glass rat chewing on a glowing crystal]`
His notes say glass rats feed on rift crystals. They don't hunt people unless their food runs out. And they love bright light.

**44** `[image: {cass} pointing his flashlight at a large cluster of purple crystals on the tunnel wall, lighting it up brightly]`
So our MC aims his flashlight at the biggest crystal cluster on the wall and turns it to full brightness.

**45** `[image: the glass rats all swarming onto the brightly lit crystal cluster, chewing happily, ignoring {cass} completely]`
Every single rat swarms the glowing crystals and starts chewing. They completely forget he exists.

**46** `[image: {cass} tiptoeing past the busy glass rats with an exaggerated careful expression, holding his breath]`
He tiptoes past an entire monster swarm, and it costs him exactly zero rift energy. Three years of boring notes just paid for themselves.

## The queen

**47** `[image: a huge underground cavern beneath the subway, a giant glowing purple crystal core floating in the middle, thick silk webs on the walls]`
At the end of the tunnel is the rift's heart. A cavern with a giant floating crystal core, the thing that keeps the whole rift open.

**48** `[image: a giant moth monster with glowing purple lantern-like wings and long feathery antennae hanging upside down above the crystal core]`
And guarding it, hanging from the ceiling, is a moth the size of a car. Its wings glow like paper lanterns.

**49** `[image: close-up of {cass}'s notebook page with a sketch of a giant moth with lantern wings and a small circle drawn on its belly]`
He knows this one. A Lantern Moth. His notes say its wings throw sleeping dust, and the only soft spot is the glowing sac under its body.

**50** `[image: the giant lantern moth spreading its glowing wings, a cloud of sparkling purple dust spilling into the air]`
The moth wakes up. It spreads its wings, and a cloud of glittering dust starts drifting toward him.

**51** `[image: {cass} pulling his hoodie up over his nose and mouth, stumbling sleepily, eyes half closed, purple dust swirling around him]`
Even through his hoodie, his eyelids get heavy. If he falls asleep here, he doesn't wake up.

**52** `[image: {cass} slapping his hand onto the glowing page of {ledger}, a glowing green hand sketch lighting up]`
He borrows Lise's healing light. One hundred and fifty more. Debt: eight hundred and eighty-eight.

**53** `[image: soft green healing light glowing around {cass}, the purple dust dissolving away from him, his eyes snapping open]`
A soft green glow washes over him. The dust melts away, and his head clears all at once.

**54** `[image: {cass} grabbing a long rusty metal rail from the tunnel floor and aiming it like a spear at the moth above]`
Now he has ten clear seconds. He picks up a broken metal rail and aims for the glowing spot his notes told him about.

**55** `[image: {cass} hurling the metal rail upward, the rail piercing the glowing sac under the giant moth's body, a burst of purple light]`
He throws it with everything he's got. Bullseye.

**56** `[image: the giant lantern moth bursting into thousands of tiny glowing purple sparks drifting through the cavern like snow]`
The Lantern Moth bursts into a thousand glowing sparks that drift down through the cavern like snow.

## Paying the bill

**57** `[image: the giant floating crystal core in the cavern pulsing brightly, cracks of light forming across its surface]`
With its guardian gone, the crystal core starts to crack. The rift is closing. Which means the core is up for grabs.

**58** `[image: {cass} pressing {ledger}'s open pages against the giant glowing crystal core, purple light pouring into the book]`
He presses the Ledger against the core, and the book drinks the whole thing.

**59** `[image: close-up of {ledger}'s page with a large glowing crimson seal stamped across it and a long column of red figures, no readable text]`
Payment received: one thousand and seventy-two. Debt: zero. And then a line he wasn't expecting.

**60** `[image: close-up of {ledger}'s page with a glowing crimson letter-grade emblem rising up from it, sparks around it, no readable text]`
Credit Rank increased. F to E. Loan time extended from ten seconds to fifteen.

**61** `[image: {cass} sitting on the cavern floor, leaning back against a rock, laughing in relief with his hands over his face]`
Our MC sits down on the cavern floor and laughs until his sides hurt. He's not in debt. For the first time in his life, he is not in debt.

**62** `[image: close-up of {ledger}'s page with a small glowing crimson number showing a surplus, no readable text]`
And there's even a little left over. One hundred and eighty-four rift energy, sitting in his account like savings.

## Someone is watching

**63** `[image: {cass} climbing out of the subway station entrance at dawn, dirty and exhausted, the city skyline behind him]`
He climbs out of the subway at dawn, covered in dust and soot, and completely happy.

**64** `[image: a small black guild surveillance drone hovering above the subway entrance, its red camera light blinking]`
He doesn't notice the small black drone hovering above the station entrance. Its camera light is blinking red.

**65** `[image: {hale} in his dark office at dawn watching a grainy security video of a lone figure leaving a subway entrance on a big screen]`
Across the city, Director Hale watches the footage on a big screen. One figure. No team. An Iron-rank rift that's suddenly gone.

**66** `[image: close-up of {hale} tapping the top of his crystal cane, a thin thoughtful smile on his face, the screen's glow on his face]`
He replays it three times. Then he smiles, and he makes a call.

**67** `[image: {auditor} standing on a rooftop at dawn, looking down at the street, writing in a small black notebook, pocket watch hanging from his coat]`
And on a rooftop above the subway entrance, the figure in the black coat writes one line in a small notebook. "Account in good standing. For now."

## The fine print

**68** `[image: {cass} back in his tiny apartment, sitting on the floor against the bed, {ledger} open on his knees, morning light]`
Back home, our MC flips through the Ledger one more time before he finally sleeps. And this time, he reads the fine print.

**69** `[image: close-up of {ledger}'s last page, glowing crimson handwriting that looks more like a contract, a blank space for a signature, no readable text]`
On the very last page, there's a section he never noticed. It's titled Collateral.

**70** `[image: close-up of glowing crimson ink forming a small sketch of a smiling man inside a picture frame on the Ledger's page]`
Under it, there's a small sketch. A smiling man in a picture frame. The same photo that sits on his desk. His father.

**71** `[image: {cass} staring at the page in shock, the crimson light on his face, his hand frozen above the page]`
And next to the sketch, one line of red ink. "If a debt is not paid, the Ledger collects what you hold most dear."

**72** `[image: {cass} slowly turning his head to look at the framed photo of a smiling man on his desk, morning light falling across it]`
Our MC looks at the photo on his desk. Then back at the book.

**73** `[image: close-up of {cass}'s face, tired but determined, crimson light reflecting in his gray eyes]`
He paid this debt on time. Next time, he might not be so lucky.

**74** `[image: {ledger} closing by itself with a soft glow, floating above the bed in the quiet apartment]`
The Ledger closes on its own, glowing softly, like it's very patient. Like it knows he'll be back.

**75** `[image: title card style image of {ledger} glowing in the dark with crimson light, a faint picture frame shape reflected in its brass corners]`
Next time on The Debt Hunter: Collateral.
